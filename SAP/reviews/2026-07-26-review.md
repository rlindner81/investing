SAP's top line keeps compounding at a high-single-digit clip: revenue grew +9.4% Y/Y in q2-2026 to \$11.24B, an acceleration from +6.0% in q1-2026 and roughly in line with the +7.7% FY-2025 pace. This is durable, cloud-led growth rather than a spike — the multi-year backlog conversion story remains intact even as the absolute rate stays modest for a name that trades on it.

The FCF profile is the standout. TTM company FCF is \$9.72B (a 22.4% margin) and even the strict ex-SBC figure is \$8.02B (18.5%). SBC of \$1.71B TTM is meaningful but well-covered — FCF quality is high and the gap between company and strict FCF is a manageable ~18%. FCF is lumpy quarter to quarter (Q1 seasonally huge at \$3.69B, Q4 weak), but the annual trend is sharply up: FY-2025 company FCF of \$9.37B nearly doubled FY-2024's \$4.80B as restructuring cash costs rolled off.

Valuation has compressed dramatically. TTM P/S sits at 4.3x and P/FCF (company) at 19.0x — versus 7.5–8.1x P/S and 43–54x P/FCF just a few quarters ago (mid-2025). The stock's ~40% de-rating from the \$267–283 highs has taken a premium software compounder down to a multiple that looks fair-to-cheap for ~9% growth plus a 22% FCF margin. Forward P/S on FY guidance is 4.4–4.5x. Even the strict ex-SBC P/FCF of 23x is undemanding for the quality.

Guidance has been steadily raised — FY revenue guide climbed from \~33B (FY-2024) to 41.3–41.9B (FY-2026) across columns, a credible raise trajectory. Balance sheet is roughly net-neutral (\$11.96B cash vs \$11.95B debt). Verdict: a rare instance of a best-in-class enterprise-software compounder trading at a reasonable multiple after a sharp, sentiment-driven drawdown.

## 3. News

**Surface narrative.** SAP reported Q2/H1-2026 after the US close on July 23. The stock enters this report deeply wounded — down ~37% YTD and roughly 49% below its summer-2025 peak, having touched a 52-week low (~€130) on July 22 right before results. The report itself was a "beat-and-trim": cloud metrics reaccelerated but the profit outlook was cut on acquisition dilution. The initial after-hours pop faded, then the stock recovered into week's end (this repo's price data shows a Jul-23 washout to ~\$146, then a bounce to \$160 by Jul 26).

**The deeper read — mostly substantiated.** The bull case rests on hard, disclosed numbers, not narrative:
- **Current cloud backlog €22.9B, +27% Y/Y (+26% cc)** — a genuine reacceleration after two softer quarters. This is a committed forward-revenue gauge, the most real metric SAP prints. *Substantiated.*
- **Cloud revenue +24% cc to €6.3B; Cloud ERP Suite +27%, now 88% of cloud** — the SaaS transition is measurably speeding up. Software licenses fell -32% as expected. *Substantiated.*
- **FCF +27% Y/Y to €3.0B in Q2; \~€10B FY FCF target reiterated.** *Substantiated.*
- **The guidance trim is small and self-inflicted:** FY non-IFRS operating profit cut to €11.8–12.2B (from €11.9–12.3B), a ~€100M reduction *entirely* from the July closings of Dremio and Prior Labs (AI/data-fabric deals, loss-making in H2). Cloud-revenue and FCF targets were held. *Substantiated — and arguably a quality signal, not deterioration.*
- **AI traction:** AI / Business Data Cloud appeared in >90% of the 50 largest deals; new wins named include Shell, Morgan Stanley, Eli Lilly, Electrolux. Directionally real but harder to independently size — partly narrative.

**Price move vs. reality.** The ~40% drawdown ran well ahead of the fundamentals, which kept compounding — this looks like a sentiment/valuation de-rate (enterprise-spend fatigue, S/4HANA migration-pace worries, an AI-acquisition spree, a July hiring freeze), not a business break. The Street stayed constructive post-print: KeyBanc reiterated Buy (€235 PT, ~19x 2026 P/E), BMO raised to \$177, Barclays and TD Cowen held positive with modest PT trims, flagging S/4HANA and AI-monetization timing as watchpoints.

**Catalysts/risks:** next print is Q3-2026 (late Oct); watch whether backlog growth decelerates as management guided for H2 (macro + Middle-East geopolitics), Dremio/Prior Labs dilution, and AI-monetization proof. €10B buyback underway (~€2.6B done). Sentiment is washed out but the downtrend is intact.

**Rating:** 1.2 — BUY

## Verdict

SAP is a high-quality enterprise-software compounder that has de-rated ~40% from its 2025 highs into a genuinely reasonable multiple: 4.3x TTM P/S and 19x P/FCF against ~9% revenue growth, a 22% FCF margin, and a reaccelerating cloud backlog (+27% Y/Y). The Q2-2026 print was a beat-and-trim — cloud and FCF strong, the small profit-guide cut entirely self-inflicted from AI acquisitions (Dremio, Prior Labs) — so the drawdown looks driven by sentiment and enterprise-spend fatigue rather than a broken business, and the Street stayed constructive. The main risk is timing: the chart is still in a clear downtrend below all major SMAs with no confirmed reversal, and management itself guided H2 backlog to decelerate on macro/geopolitical caution. A confirmed base above the 158–161 POC and evidence that AI acquisitions convert to revenue (not just dilution) would firm up the rating; a further backlog-growth slowdown or margin slippage would undercut the value case.

**Rating:** 0.7 — BUY
