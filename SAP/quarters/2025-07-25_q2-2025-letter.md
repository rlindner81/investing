Financial Highlights1



Second Quarter 2025



In the second quarter, current cloud backlog grew by 22% to &euro;18.05



billion and was up 28% at constant currencies. Cloud revenue was up 24% to &euro;5.13 billion and up 28% at constant currencies. Cloud



ERP Suite revenue was up 30% to &euro;4.42 billion and up 34% at constant currencies.



Software licenses revenue decreased by 15% to &euro;0.19 billion and



was down 13% at constant currencies. Cloud and software revenue was up 11% to &euro;7.97 billion and up 14% at constant currencies. Services



revenue was down 5% to &euro;1.06 billion and down 2% at constant currencies. Total revenue was up 9% to &euro;9.03 billion and up 12%



at constant currencies.



The share of more predictable revenue increased by 2 percentage points



to 86%.



IFRS cloud gross profit was up 26% to &euro;3.83 billion. Non-IFRS cloud



gross profit was up 27% to &euro;3.86 billion and was up 31% at constant currencies. IFRS Cloud gross margin was up 1.8 percentage points



to 74.7%, non-IFRS cloud gross margin up 1.9 percentage points to 75.2% and up 1.8 percentage points at constant currencies to 75.0%.



IFRS operating profit increased to &euro;2.46 billion and IFRS operating



margin was up 12.5 percentage points to 27.2%. IFRS operating profit growth was positively impacted by a restructuring expense decline



of &euro;0.6 billion as compared to Q2 2024 in connection with the 2024 transformation program. Non-IFRS operating profit was up 32%



to &euro;2.57 billion and was up 35% at constant currencies, non-IFRS operating margin increased by 5.0 percentage points to 28.5% and



was up 4.8 percentage points to 28.2% at constant currencies. Both, IFRS and non-IFRS operating profit growth benefitted from the



operational efficiencies realized through successful execution of the 2024 transformation program and lower share-based compensation



expenses.



Driven by the operating profit growth, IFRS earnings per share (basic)



increased 91% to &euro;1.45. Non-IFRS earnings per share (basic) increased 37% to &euro;1.50. IFRS effective tax rate was 30.1% and non-IFRS



effective tax rate was 30.8%. Both were mainly driven by a

Share Repurchase Program



In May 2023, SAP announced a share repurchase program with an aggregate



volume of up to &euro;5 billion and a term until December 31, 2025. As of June 30, 2025, SAP had repurchased 24,743,442 shares



at an average price of &euro;185.51 resulting in a purchased volume of approximately &euro;4.6 billion under the program.



2024 Transformation Program: Focus on scalability of operations



and key strategic growth areas



In January 2024, SAP announced a company-wide restructuring program



which concluded as planned in the first quarter 2025. Overall expenses associated with the program were approximately &euro;3.2 billion.



Restructuring payouts amounted to &euro;2.5 billion for the full-year 2024 and &euro;0.6



billion for the first six months of 2025. Approximately &euro;0.2 billion is expected to be paid out in the remainder of 2025.
