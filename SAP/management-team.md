# SAP SE (NYSE: SAP) — Management Team

SAP is led by a two-tier German board structure: an **Executive Board** (Vorstand) that runs the company day to day, and a **Supervisory Board** (Aufsichtsrat) that oversees and appoints it. Co-founder Hasso Plattner chaired the Supervisory Board for decades and remains the company's defining figure historically.

---

## Executive Board

### Christian Klein — Chief Executive Officer

**Background:**
- Born 1980 in Germany; joined SAP in 1999 as a student and never left — a true SAP "lifer."
- Studied business information technology (Wirtschaftsinformatik); rose through controlling and operations roles.
- Became COO, then was named co-CEO in October 2019 alongside Jennifer Morgan, and **sole CEO in April 2020** — at 39, one of the youngest CEOs of a major global company.

**Role at SAP:**
- Architect of SAP's cloud transformation: made "RISE with SAP" and the S/4HANA Cloud migration the company's central strategy, and drove the 2024 transformation program that restructured the workforce toward cloud and AI.
- Repositioned SAP around **Business AI** (Joule, Business Data Cloud) as the next growth and pricing lever.
- Presents the strategic framing on earnings calls; oversaw the near-doubling of free cash flow in FY2025.

---

### Dominik Asam — Chief Financial Officer

**Background:**
- Joined SAP as CFO in March 2023, succeeding Luka Mucic.
- Previously CFO of Airbus (2019–2022) and, before that, CFO of Infineon Technologies — a deep background in large-cap German industrial/technology finance.
- Engineering and finance training (studied mechanical engineering; MBA-level finance career).

**Role at SAP:**
- Primary financial spokesperson on earnings calls; owns guidance, capital allocation, and the cost/cash-flow discipline narrative.
- Oversaw the financial execution of the 2024 transformation program and the resulting margin and FCF step-up; guided the new up-to-€10B, two-year buyback announced with Q4 2025.
- Emphasizes constant-currency growth and free-cash-flow conversion as the core value-creation metrics.

(Executive Board composition changes over time; the Product & Engineering, Customer Services & Delivery, Finance, People, and Strategy portfolios are the durable pillars under CEO Klein.)

## Supervisory Board & Founders

### Hasso Plattner — Co-Founder, former Supervisory Board Chairman

- One of the five founders (1972) and the driving intellectual force behind SAP for its entire history; personally championed the in-memory HANA database that underpins S/4HANA.
- Chaired the Supervisory Board from 2003 until **May 2024**, when he stepped down after more than five decades with the company — the end of an era.
- Remains a major shareholder and SAP's most identified figure.

### Pekka Ala-Pietilä — Supervisory Board Chairman

- Succeeded Hasso Plattner as Chairman of the Supervisory Board in 2024.
- Former Nokia president and a veteran European technology board member; provides independent oversight of the Executive Board in the two-tier structure.

## Corporate Governance Notes

- **Two-tier board (German model).** The Executive Board (Vorstand) manages; the Supervisory Board (Aufsichtsrat) — half shareholder representatives, half employee representatives under German co-determination — appoints and supervises it. This differs structurally from the unitary US board.
- **Single share class.** SAP has one class of ordinary shares (1,229M issued), one vote each — no dual-class founder control. Voting influence comes from economic ownership (founders' families and foundations remain large holders).
- **Treasury-funded compensation.** SAP funds share-based compensation by buying back and reissuing treasury shares rather than issuing new stock, so the issued share count is essentially constant year to year; net shares outstanding drift only with treasury activity.
- **Capital return.** Long history of growing dividends plus multi-year buyback programs (a €5B program through 2025; a new up-to-€10B, two-year program announced with Q4 2025).
- **Foreign private issuer.** SAP files an annual 20-F and furnishes quarterly results on 6-K; it is not subject to US 10-Q/8-K quarterly reporting, so quarterly financial detail comes from its press-release "Quarterly Statement" documents.
