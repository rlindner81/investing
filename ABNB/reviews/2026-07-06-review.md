**Revenue trend:** ABNB delivered \$12.2B in FY-2025 (+10.3% Y/Y), a slight deceleration from FY-2024's +11.9%, but Q1-2026 came in at \$2.7B (+17.9% Y/Y) — the strongest quarterly growth in over a year, suggesting a meaningful re-acceleration that the market may not yet fully price in. The revenue cadence is heavily seasonal, with Q3 (summer travel) generating roughly 3x Q1.

**FCF profile:** ABNB is fully asset-light — PP&E capex is essentially zero, so company FCF equals operating cash flow exactly (\$4.6B TTM). The SBC drag is material: \$1.6B TTM reduces strict ex-SBC FCF to \$2.9B — a 36% haircut. That said, the share count has been declining steadily from 636M (Q1-2024) to 595M (Q1-2026), confirming meaningful buybacks absorb the dilution. FCF Y/Y was -4.5% in Q1-2026 on its own, though the trailing full year improved +2.8%.

**Current valuation:** At \$148.35, ABNB trades at 7.0x TTM P/S and 19.3x P/FCF (company), or 30.2x on the more conservative strict ex-SBC basis. The forward P/S on NQ guidance is ~6.7x — not cheap, but not stretched for a business compounding revenue at ~10–18% with a \$4.6B FCF engine and \$4.6B net cash cushion. Compared to recent history (P/S ranged 5.7x–9.8x over the past two years), 7.0x sits near the middle of the range.

**Guidance trajectory:** ABNB only issues quantitative next-quarter guidance; full-year is qualitative. Beat track record is consistent and tight: beats of 0.9–3.3% every reported quarter. The Q2-2026 guide of \$3,540–3,600M implies ~15% Y/Y growth, which would confirm the Q1 acceleration was durable.

**Balance sheet:** Net cash of \$4.6B (no meaningful debt) with ~\$88B market cap — roughly 5% of market cap in cash. SBC at \$1.6B TTM is notable but partially offset by buybacks reducing float ~6% over two years.

**Verdict:** ABNB is a high-quality, asset-light travel platform re-accelerating to mid-teens growth while trading near the middle of its two-year valuation range — fair to modestly attractive if the Q1 acceleration holds.
