**Revenue** grew +14.3% Y/Y in Q3 to \$1.72B, down from Q2's +18.9% and still above the 2025 run-rate of \~+10%. TTM revenue is \$6.57B. The Q4 guide of \$1.950–1.985B implies +13.6% to +15.6%, so growth is holding in the mid-teens. The mix is the weak spot: advertising grew only +4%, and Other Revenue (+84%, subscriptions) generated roughly three quarters of the incremental dollars. Growth is real, but the ad engine is close to stalling.

**FCF** is strongly positive and inflecting. TTM company FCF is \$777M (+88% Y/Y), the 11.8% TTM margin is up from 7.4% for FY-2025, and each of the last five quarters has grown FCF triple digits or near it. The caveat is SBC. At \$1.03B TTM it exceeds company FCF, so strict FCF ex-SBC is still -\$249M (-3.8% margin), although that gap has narrowed from -\$822M in FY-2024. The fully diluted count is +3.8% Y/Y. Management's promised flat 2027 share count is what turns FCF into FCF per share, and that program has not been sized.

**Valuation** is undemanding: 1.5x TTM P/S and 13.1x P/FCF at \$5.92, with forward P/S on the Q4 guide also \~1.5x. At the \~\$5.25 after-hours level, those multiples fall to \~1.35x and \~11.6x. That is cheap for mid-teens growth and an FCF line compounding at this rate, and the multiple has compressed from 3.6–4.4x in 2024 even as FCF went from break-even to \$0.78B.

**Guidance track record:** Snap beat the top of its own range five quarters running, then landed at +1.3% over the midpoint in Q3, inside the range. The Q4 guide midpoint is slightly above the \~\$1.95B consensus. Q3 EBITDA beat the guide top by \$33M, and the infrastructure guide was cut.

**Balance sheet:** \$2.77B of cash plus marketable securities against \$3.49B of debt leaves net debt of \~\$0.7B. That is manageable at \~\$1.4B TTM adj. EBITDA, and the converts are being retired steadily.

**Verdict:** cheap on company FCF with real operating leverage, but the SBC burden and decelerating ad revenue keep it from being clearly undervalued.

**Rating:** +0.5 — BUY

## 3. News

**Surface narrative (last 2–4 weeks).** The story is Q3 earnings. SNAP fell \~10–11% after hours on Nov 4 ([GuruFocus](https://www.gurufocus.com/news/9171660/snap-reports-q3-earnings-stock-drops-over-10-snap), [Benzinga](https://www.benzinga.com/markets/earnings/26/11/62893632/snap-stock-dives-after-q3-earnings-heres-why)) on a small revenue miss (\$1.722B vs. \~\$1.73B LSEG) and DAU of 495M vs. \~499M expected. Headlines combined the DAU miss with Snap's decision to stop reporting DAU from Q1 2027 ([TechBuzz](https://techbuzz.ai/articles/snap-shares-crater-10-on-q3-revenue-user-growth-miss)). Two days earlier, Snap settled the Los Angeles teen social-media-addiction bellwether just before opening arguments, with Spiegel expected to testify ([CBS](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/slug-snap-settles-social-media-addiction-lawsuit/)). In October, analyst notes were mostly Holds (RBC, Goldman, Wells Fargo) plus a UBS target raise to \$7.

**Deeper read.**
- **Earnings miss: substantiated, but small.** The 8-K shows revenue at +1.3% above the midpoint of Snap's own guide. The more important hard fact is that advertising grew only +4%, which management confirmed on the call ("below our ambitions"), with brand/CPG declining. Offsetting it, also substantiated: GAAP net income of \$56M, adj. EBITDA of \$383M vs. \~\$337M consensus, TTM FCF of \$777M, and a Q4 guide midpoint (\~\$1.97B) above the \~\$1.95B consensus.
- **DAU → MAU switch: real data, debatable framing.** It is a disclosed reporting change. NA DAU of 92M is -6% Y/Y and has been flat for three quarters, so dropping the metric is reasonably read as removing a weak scorecard. Meanwhile MAU (977M, +4%) keeps growing.
- **Litigation settlement: substantiated, with undisclosed terms.** It removes the immediate risk of a jury trial and CEO testimony. The school-district trials (Feb 2027) and the next bellwethers (Mar 2027) are still ahead, and a 2025 securities class action is pending. The overall liability is still unknown.
- **Analyst targets: mostly attention.** Consensus is \~\$7.4–7.7 with a Hold rating. No post-print revisions were visible yet.

**Price moves.** The +9.8% run from \$5.39 to \$5.92 into the print (Nov 2–4), on 2x volume, came ahead of any new data. It was helped by the settlement and by a broad risk-on tape after the midterms. The after-hours drop reversed it. The move was positioning, and the print didn't justify it.

**Catalysts.** Specs ship "later this fall," the first hardware revenue but immaterial. Q4 earnings come in early Feb 2027, together with the sizing of the 2027 dilution program, which is the key one. Feb–Mar 2027 brings the next addiction trials. Q1 2027 is the first print on the new MAU/ARPU metrics.

**Sentiment.** Short interest is 6.7–7.5% of float, a two-year high. Sell-side is firmly neutral. The retail narrative is "cheap but no conviction" ([Seeking Alpha](https://seekingalpha.com/article/4962607-snap-q3-the-potential-is-there-the-conviction-isnt)).

**Key risks.** Ad growth could stall if brand doesn't recover in 2027. Subscription growth is masking that. Youth-safety regulation is spreading (Turkey's under-15 ban, US age assurance) and could hit engagement. SBC is \~\$1B a year, and Specs spend is open-ended.

**Rating:** -0.4 — HOLD

## Verdict

Snap's fundamentals keep improving where it counts: first GAAP profit, 22% EBITDA margin, TTM FCF up 88% to \$777M, and a Q4 guide implying mid-teens growth. All of that at \~1.4x sales and \~12x FCF after the after-hours drop. The problem is quality. Advertising grew just 4%, subscriptions are carrying the top line, and dropping DAU reporting looks like dodging a weak metric. The stock's pre-print run has fully unwound, and the chart breaks below its 50d and 200d SMAs. Net, it's a HOLD. Ad growth reaccelerating to high single digits in Q4, plus a sized buyback that holds the fully diluted count flat in 2027, would move it to BUY. Ad growth below 4% or a large litigation charge would move it to SELL.

**Rating:** -0.2 — HOLD
