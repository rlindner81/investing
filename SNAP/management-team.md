# Snap Inc. (NYSE: SNAP) — Management Team

## Executive Leadership

---

### Evan Spiegel — Co-Founder & Chief Executive Officer

**Background:**
- Born June 4, 1990; grew up in Pacific Palisades, Los Angeles (an affluent coastal neighborhood)
- Father: John Spiegel (partner at Munger, Tolles & Olson LLP); mother: Melissa Thomas (professional)
- Attended Crossroads School for Arts & Sciences, Santa Monica
- Enrolled at Stanford University in 2009 in the product design program (focused on human-computer interaction); left in 2012 without graduating, a few months before receiving his degree

**Founding Snapchat:**
- In 2011, co-created "Picaboo" — the ephemeral photo messaging app — with Reggie Brown and Bobby Murphy at Stanford; renamed Snapchat later that year
- Reggie Brown was pushed out of the company in 2013 and later reached a settlement (~$157.5M) with Snap
- Famously rejected a $3B acquisition offer from Mark Zuckerberg in November 2013
- In September 2016, renamed the company Snap Inc. and introduced the Spectacles hardware product — a deliberate signal that Snap is a camera company, not just an app
- Led Snap's IPO in March 2017 at $17/share; became one of the youngest CEOs to take a major company public (age 26)

**Leadership style and vision:**
- Known for secretive product development; Snap rarely previews features before launch
- Long-term thesis centers on augmented reality as the successor to the smartphone as the primary computing interface
- Has moved aggressively on AR hardware (Spectacles, multiple generations) even as the product has not commercially succeeded
- Married Miranda Kerr (model/entrepreneur) in 2017; they have two sons together

**Governance:**
- Holds Class C shares (10 votes per share) alongside Bobby Murphy, giving the two founders majority voting control regardless of economic ownership
- As of 2024, Evan Spiegel owns approximately 3% economic interest but holds majority voting power

---

### Bobby Murphy — Co-Founder & Chief Technology Officer

**Background:**
- Born June 14, 1988 in Los Angeles
- Parents: Filipino immigrant father, Irish-American mother
- Attended Pacific Palisades Charter High School
- Studied mathematics and computational mathematics at Stanford University (class of 2012)
- Met Evan Spiegel through the Kappa Sigma fraternity at Stanford

**Role at Snap:**
- Built the original Snapchat iOS application; served as the technical engine behind the founding team
- Has maintained a deliberately low public profile compared to Spiegel; rarely speaks on earnings calls or gives media interviews
- Leads all technology and engineering strategy as CTO, including infrastructure, AR platform, ML systems, and Snap OS

**Governance:**
- Holds Class C shares alongside Spiegel; the two together maintain majority voting control
- Economic ownership approximately 9-10% as of 2024

### Derek Andersen — Chief Financial Officer

**Background:**
- Joined Snap as CFO in September 2017
- Undergraduate degree from the University of Southern California; MBA from USC Marshall School of Business
- Prior to Snap: CFO at Revel Systems (iPad-based point-of-sale platform); before that, investment banking at Deutsche Bank and Lehman Brothers in the technology, media, and telecom coverage groups

**Role at Snap:**
- Has overseen Snap's financial strategy through the company's full post-IPO history: the hypergrowth phase (2018–2021), the 2022 advertising collapse, and the 2023–2024 recovery
- Primary financial spokesperson on all earnings calls; presents the detailed financial results alongside Evan Spiegel's strategic framing
- Led cost-reduction initiatives in 2023–2024 that returned Snap to positive Adjusted EBITDA and, for the first time, positive free cash flow
- Manages investor relations, capital markets, and all debt/equity financing activity (Snap has issued multiple convertible notes to fund operations)

### Rob Wilk — Chief Business Officer

**Background:**
- Joined Snap circa 2023
- Previously served as Vice President of Advertising Sales at Amazon (Amazon Ads), where he helped build one of the fastest-growing digital ad businesses in the industry
- Deep relationships with major advertisers and agency holding companies from his Amazon tenure

**Role at Snap:**
- Leads all revenue-generating commercial functions: advertiser sales, agency partnerships, and DR advertising growth
- Central to Snap's effort to grow direct response advertising (which has better unit economics and more scalability than brand advertising)
- Works on improving advertiser ROI through better measurement tools (Snap Pixel, Conversions API) and advanced bidding products

## Corporate Governance Notes

- **Dual-class shares:** Evan Spiegel and Bobby Murphy hold Class C shares with 10 votes per share, giving them permanent majority voting control. Class A shares (NYSE: SNAP) carry 1 vote per share; Class B shares (held by early employees and insiders) carry 1 vote per share as well. This structure has drawn consistent criticism from institutional investors and proxy advisors, but is common among founder-led technology companies.
- **Reggie Brown settlement:** The third original co-founder, Reggie Brown, was pushed out in 2013 and reached a ~$157.5M cash settlement with the company. He is not a shareholder or employee.
- **Headquarters:** Santa Monica, California. Snap previously committed to keeping its HQ in Los Angeles County as part of its identity as a non-Silicon-Valley tech company.
- **Workforce reductions:** Snap executed significant layoffs in August 2022 (~20% of workforce) and September 2023 (~10% of workforce) to reduce operating costs and improve the path to profitability.
