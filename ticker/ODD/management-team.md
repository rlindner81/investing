## Corporate Governance Notes

- **Dual-class shares:** Oran and Shiran hold Class B shares with superior voting rights, retaining effective control of the company despite the public float. This has drawn governance criticism from proxy advisors but gives the founding team long-term strategic autonomy.
- **Sibling co-founder dynamic:** Unusually, both the CEO (strategic/financial vision) and CPO (product/technology vision) are from the same family — concentrating institutional knowledge but also creating succession risk.
- **Headquarters:** Tel Aviv, Israel with significant U.S. operations (Oddity Labs, Boston; U.S. commercial operations)
- **Incorporated:** Cayman Islands (standard for Israeli tech companies listed in the U.S.)
