# ServiceNow, Inc. (NYSE: NOW) — Management Team

## Executive Leadership

---

### Bill McDermott — Chairman & Chief Executive Officer

**Background:**
- Born 1961 in Amityville, New York (Long Island); a well-known account of his early
  entrepreneurialism has him buying and running a delicatessen as a teenager to fund
  college.
- Undergraduate degree from Dowling College; MBA from the Kellogg School of Management,
  Northwestern University.
- Early career at Xerox, where he rose to become the company's youngest corporate
  officer and division president, building a reputation as an elite enterprise
  salesperson.

**Career before ServiceNow:**
- Spent roughly a decade at **SAP**, ultimately serving as sole **CEO of SAP** (2014–2019),
  the first American to lead the German enterprise-software giant. Grew SAP's cloud
  business and revenue substantially during his tenure.
- Authored the business memoir *Winners Dream*.

**Role at ServiceNow:**
- Became **President and CEO in 2019**, succeeding John Donahoe; later added the
  **Chairman** title.
- Has driven ServiceNow's evolution from an IT-workflow vendor into a broad enterprise
  "platform of platforms" and, most recently, into an AI-first "control tower"
  positioning. Presides over the company's aggressive 2030 targets ($30B+ subscription
  revenue, Rule of 60+).
- Known for a high-energy, sales-led leadership style and a strong external/investor
  presence; delivers the strategic framing on earnings calls.

---

### Gina Mastantuono — President & Chief Financial Officer

**Background:**
- Joined ServiceNow as **CFO in 2020**; later also given the **President** title,
  reflecting an expanded operating remit.
- Prior to ServiceNow: CFO of **Ingram Micro**, the global technology distribution and
  supply-chain company; earlier senior finance roles at Revlon and other companies.

**Role at ServiceNow:**
- Primary financial spokesperson on earnings calls; presents results and — critically —
  the **subscription-revenue and cRPO guidance** that the market trades on (ServiceNow
  guides subscription revenue, next-quarter and full-year, not total revenue).
- Oversaw the company's shift in capital structure in 2026, when ServiceNow raised ~$8B
  in debt (senior notes, term loan, commercial paper) to fund the Armis and Veza
  acquisitions — a departure from its historically near-debt-free balance sheet.
- Champions the "Rule of X" framing (constant-currency subscription growth + FCF margin)
  and the committed reduction of stock-based compensation to <10% of revenue by 2029.

## Corporate Governance Notes

- **Single share class.** Unlike many founder-led technology companies, ServiceNow has a
  single class of common stock with one vote per share — no dual-class/super-voting
  structure. Fred Luddy, the founder, stepped back from an operating role years ago and
  the company is professionally managed.
- **Board.** Bill McDermott serves as Chairman of the Board in addition to CEO; the board
  is otherwise composed of independent directors typical of a large-cap S&P 500 company.
- **5-for-1 stock split (December 17, 2025).** The first stock split in company history,
  executed to improve share accessibility after the stock reached ~$1,000 pre-split. All
  per-share figures in filings are retroactively adjusted; the market price and share
  counts in this repo are on the post-split basis.
- **Capital allocation.** ServiceNow runs an active share-repurchase program (buybacks
  offset a meaningful portion of SBC dilution) and, beginning in 2026, took on
  acquisition-funding debt for the first time at scale (Armis, Veza).
- **Executive continuity.** The McDermott–Mastantuono leadership pairing has been in place
  since 2019–2020, providing consistent strategic and financial messaging through the
  company's AI repositioning and the 2025–2026 multiple de-rating.
