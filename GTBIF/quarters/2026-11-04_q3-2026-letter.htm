Green Thumb Industries Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results

 

CHICAGO and VANCOUVER, British Columbia, November 4, 2026 – Green Thumb Industries Inc. (“Green Thumb” or the “Company”) (CSE: GTII) (OTCQX: GTBIF), a leading national cannabis company with a portfolio of best-in-class brands and owner of RISE Dispensaries, today reported its financial results for the quarter ended September 30, 2026. Financial results are reported in accordance with U.S. generally accepted accounting principles (“GAAP”), and all currency is in U.S. dollars.

 

Highlights for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026:

•
Revenue of $324.4 million, an increase of 11.3% over the same period in the prior year.
•
Cash at quarter end totaled $316.0 million.
•
GAAP net loss of $4.8 million or $0.02 per basic and diluted share.
•
Normalized EBITDA of $89.4 million or 27.6% of revenue.
•
Cash flow from operations of $58.3 million.
•
Added six RISE Dispensaries across three states: Port Charlotte and Port St. Lucie in Florida; Sparks in Nevada; and Hanover, West Mifflin, and Allentown in Pennsylvania.
•
Entered into an amendment with RYTHM, Inc. (“RYM”) on August 10, 2026, that removed the beneficial ownership limitation on certain of the Company’s RYM convertible notes and pre-funded warrants, effective October 10, 2026. As a result, the Company will consolidate RYM in its financial statements beginning on that date1.

 

1See “RYTHM, INC. Consolidation” below.

 

Recent Developments

 

•
Expanded to 27 Florida retail locations with the opening of RISE Dispensaries in Cape Coral, Belleview, and Mulberry.
•
Received U.S. Drug Enforcement Administration (“DEA”) registrations for certain state medical cannabis licenses as part of the DEA’s initial issuance, following the federal rescheduling of medical cannabis to Schedule III under the Controlled Substances Act.

This performance is driven by the strength of our brands, the execution of our team, and our commitment to operating with financial discipline. Our financial strength enables us to stay patient and move decisively as the market develops.”

Third Quarter 2026 Financial Overview

 

Total revenue for the third quarter 2026 was $324.4 million, up 11.3% from the prior year period. Revenue growth in the third quarter was driven primarily by retail sales in Minnesota, reflecting the launch of adult-use sales in the state on September 17, 2025, as well as continued growth in existing markets, particularly Florida, New Jersey, New York, Ohio and Virginia, as well as the impact of newly opened and acquired retail stores since September 30, 2025. The increase was partially offset by price compression and increased competition in select markets.

 

Retail revenue increased 10.1% versus the third quarter of 2025. Third quarter 2026 comparable sales (stores open at least 12 months) increased 3.0% versus the prior year on a base of 108 stores. Comparable sales returned to growth for the first time since the third quarter of 2024, improving from an approximately 1% decline in the second quarter of 2026.

 

Consumer Packaged Goods revenue increased 16.0% versus the third quarter of 2025, primarily due to the launch of adult-use sales in Minnesota, as well as continued growth in existing markets, particularly in Illinois, New Jersey, New York, and Ohio, partially offset by price compression and increased competition.

 

Gross profit for the third quarter 2026 was $146.8 million or 45.2% of revenue, up from $144.0 million or 49.4% of revenue over the prior year period. Excluding the RYTHM brand licensing fees of $15.8 million incurred in the current period, gross profit would have been $162.5 million or 50.1% of revenue, mainly driven by the launch of adult-use sales in Minnesota as well as continued growth in existing markets as discussed above.

 

Total selling, general and administrative expenses for the third quarter 2026 were $121.7 million or 37.5% of revenue, compared to $107.3 million or 36.8% of revenue for the third quarter 2025. The increase in selling, general, and administrative expenses was primarily attributable to increased compensation and benefits costs, reflecting both the Company’s increased store count and planned changes to its compensation structure intended to support retention and execution.

Total other income (expense) for the third quarter 2026 was ($3.7) million compared to $36.2 million in the third quarter of 2025. The reduction in other income (expense) was primarily attributable to the gain on sale of Green Thumb's intellectual property and hemp business to RYTHM, Inc. in the prior year.

Income tax expense for the third quarter of 2026 was $28.1 million compared to $49.1 million for the comparable period in the prior year. The decrease in income tax expense was primarily due to the Department of Justice's final order reclassifying state legal medical cannabis to Schedule III.

 

Net loss attributable to the Company for the third quarter 2026 was $4.8 million or $0.02 per basic and diluted share, compared to net income of $23.3 million, or $0.10 per basic and diluted share in the prior year period, due to the gain on sale of intellectual property in the prior year period.

 

In the third quarter 2026, EBITDA was $59.3 million or 18.3% of revenue, versus $66.8 million or 22.9% of revenue for the comparable prior year period. Normalized EBITDA, which excludes licensing fees of $15.8 million, non-cash stock-based compensation of $10.4 million and other non-operating adjustments of $4.0 million, was $89.4 million or 27.6% of revenue, up from $80.2 million or 27.5% of revenue for the third quarter 2025.

Balance Sheet and Liquidity

 

As of September 30, 2026, current assets were $661.4 million, including cash and cash equivalents of $316.0 million. Total debt outstanding was $277.3 million, which includes $177.0 million of senior debt.

 

Total basic and diluted weighted average shares outstanding for the three months ended September 30, 2026, were 218.6 million shares.

Capital Allocation

 

Since launching its first repurchase program in 2023, the Company has repurchased the equivalent of approximately 29.5 million Subordinate Voting Shares for $203.4 million, with an average price of $6.90 per share. RYTHM, Inc. Consolidation

 

On August 10, 2026, subsidiaries of the Company entered into an amendment with RYM that, effective October 10, 2026, removed the 49.99% beneficial ownership limitation on certain of the Company’s RYM convertible notes and pre-funded warrants, including pre-funded warrants that may be issued under the Company’s shared services agreement with RYM. As a result, the Company will consolidate RYM in its financial statements beginning on that date. The consolidation results solely from the amended terms and does not involve any purchase of RYM securities or change in the Company’s ownership interest in RYM; the Company does not currently intend to exercise any of the securities. RYM was accounted for under the equity method in the third quarter, and results beginning in the fourth quarter will not be directly comparable to prior periods.

About Green Thumb Industries

 

Green Thumb Industries Inc. (“Green Thumb” or the “Company”) is a leading national cannabis consumer packaged goods company and retailer headquartered in Chicago, Illinois. The company manufactures and distributes a portfolio of branded cannabis products, including RYTHM, Dogwalkers, incredibles, Beboe, &Shine, Doctor Solomon’s and Good Green. Green Thumb also owns and operates RISE Dispensaries, a rapidly growing national retail chain. Green Thumb serves millions of patients and customers each year with a mission to promote well-being through the power of cannabis while giving back to the communities it serves. Established in 2014, Green Thumb has manufacturing facilities and retail stores across 14 U.S. markets, employing approximately 5,000 people. More information is available at www.gtigrows.com.
