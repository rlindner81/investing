## 3. News & Catalysts

**Q1-2026 earnings (May 6, 2026).** Green Thumb reported Q1 revenue of \$300.2M (+7.4% Y/Y), GAAP net income of \$15.4M (\$0.07/share), and normalized EBITDA of \$93.5M at a 31.2% margin. Company FCF was \$56.9M for the quarter, up 28.3% Y/Y, and the balance sheet turned net cash positive (\$55M) for the first time. The CFO issued qualitative guidance: Q2-2026 revenue expected "flat to down slightly sequentially" — consistent with the established pattern of soft sequential color rather than numeric ranges.

**DEA rescheduling — Schedule III (April 23, 2026).** The DEA finalized the rescheduling of cannabis from Schedule I to Schedule III, effective for products covered by FDA approval or state medical marijuana licenses. This is the most significant regulatory development in the US cannabis industry in a generation. The immediate impact: state medical cannabis operators are no longer subject to Section 280E of the Internal Revenue Code. Green Thumb filed DEA registration applications for its state-licensed medical operations in May 2026 to position itself to benefit from tax relief. The company estimates meaningful annual tax savings, with the benefit flowing progressively through future quarters as operations qualify.

**Share buybacks.** Green Thumb has been actively returning capital: shares outstanding declined from ~236M a year ago to ~225.9M in Q1-2026. The buyback program reflects management's confidence at current share prices and provides a per-share earnings tailwind even if top-line growth remains modest.

**280E relief — scale of impact.** Estimates suggest 280E relief saves multistate operators (MSOs) hundreds of millions annually in aggregate. For Green Thumb specifically, given its ~31% EBITDA margin and the portion of revenue from medical sales, the tax benefit is expected to be material — though the exact dollar figure will depend on what share of revenues qualify under state medical licenses.
