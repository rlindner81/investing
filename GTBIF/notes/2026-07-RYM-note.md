# GTI / RYTHM Inc. (RYM) — Structure & Control Note

**Date:** July 2026

---

## The Story

GTI's brand IP — RYTHM, incredibles, Beboe, Dogwalkers, Doctor Solomon's, Shine,
Good Green — is now housed in a separate Nasdaq-listed entity called RYTHM Inc.
(RYM). GTI still runs the actual cannabis business (cultivation, manufacturing,
retail) and licenses its own brands back from RYM. This is deliberate staging for
federal legalization.

The logic: when cannabis is federally rescheduled, the big payoff is listing on a
major US exchange. GTI as a plant-touching operator cannot do that today. But RYM
is already on Nasdaq — it is federally clean, holds the brand IP, and has Ben
Kovler at the helm. When legalization comes, GTI converts its notes and open-market
position to just over 50% control of RYM, executes a board-approved reverse merger
(Ben chairs both boards), and the combined entity lists as an operator-plus-brand
business. The expensive work — accumulating ~33% equity and ~$72M in convertible
notes that get them to just under 50% — is all done at pre-legalization prices.

The vehicle was not designed from scratch. Agrify Corporation was a distressed
cannabis equipment company with an existing Nasdaq listing that GTI effectively
took over in late 2024 and renamed RYTHM Inc. The window was available, the price
was right, and the timing preceded any legalization catalyst.

Neither party can credibly hurt the other: RYM earns 100% of its revenue from
GTI licensing fees; GTI would have to rebrand from scratch without RYM's licenses.
The mutual hostage dynamic is intentional — it locks in the arrangement until GTI
is ready to consolidate.

## Structure

RYTHM Inc. (Nasdaq: RYM, formerly Agrify Corporation, CIK 0001800637) is a
federally compliant, non-plant-touching IP holding company. GTI sold all brand IP
into RYM across two transactions in 2025:

- **May 20, 2025** — incredibles brand IP acquired by RYM; GTI licenses it back
  via Incredibles License Agreement (revenue-based fee)
- **August 27, 2025** — RYTHM, Beboe, Dogwalkers, Doctor Solomon's, Shine, and
  Good Green IP sold to RYM; GTI licenses all back via fixed annual fees
  (amended from revenue-based to fixed, effective April 1, 2026)

Ben Kovler chairs both GTI and RYM. GTI provides management and operational
support to RYM under shared services agreements. As of Q1-2026, GTI held
approximately **33% of RYM's outstanding shares**, accounted for under the equity
method (carrying value ~$180M on GTI's balance sheet).

## Convertible Notes — Three Tranches

All notes issued to RSLGH, LLC, an indirect wholly-owned GTI subsidiary.

| Tranche | Issued | Principal | Conversion price | Maturity |
|---------|--------|-----------|-----------------|----------|
| Nov 2024 | 2024-11-05 | undisclosed | **$3.158 / share** | Nov 5, 2025 (amended) |
| May 2025 | 2025-05-22 | $30M aggregate (GTI + co-investors) | **$23.53 / share** | 2026-11-22 |
| Aug 2025 | 2025-08-25 | **$50M** | **$29.475 / share** | 2027-02-25 |

Total notes outstanding: **$72M** as of March 31, 2026 (per GTI Q1-2026 10-Q).  
Interest rate: 10% per annum on all tranches.  
All notes convertible into common stock **or pre-funded warrants** at holder's election.

**Beneficial ownership cap on conversion:**
- RSLGH (GTI): **49.99%** — specifically carved out vs. the standard 4.99% for all other investors
- All other note holders: 4.99%

Conversion of any tranche requires Nasdaq Rule 5635 shareholder approval if the
resulting issuance exceeds 20% of outstanding shares.

## Path to Majority Control

The 49.99% beneficial ownership cap is not a meaningful ceiling. The gap from
49.99% to 50.01% is ~0.02% of shares outstanding. At any conceivable market cap
— even a post-legalization rally — buying that rounding error costs trivially
little and requires only one willing seller on any given day.

1. Convert notes → reach 49.99% (contractually permitted, already funded)
2. Buy ~0.02% of shares on the open market → cross 50.01%
3. Ben approves a merger from both boards simultaneously

The Nov 2024 note at **$3.158/share** is particularly favorable: if its principal
is meaningful, those shares are acquired at a fraction of any legalized market price,
significantly diluting other RYM shareholders at GTI's benefit.
