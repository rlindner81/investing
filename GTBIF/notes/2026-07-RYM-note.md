# GTI / RYTHM Inc. (RYM) — Structure & Control Note

**Date:** July 2026 (updated October 2026)

---

## The Story

GTI's brand IP — RYTHM, incredibles, Beboe, Dogwalkers, Doctor Solomon's, Shine,
Good Green, Señorita — is housed in a separate Nasdaq-listed entity called RYTHM
Inc. (RYM). GTI still runs the actual cannabis business (cultivation, manufacturing,
retail) and licenses its own brands back from RYM.

**RYM is not the listing vehicle.** On the Q2-2026 call Kovler was asked directly
why RYM shouldn't simply acquire GTI after rescheduling, and rejected it: *"That's
not a very fast path. There's complicated tax ramifications of all those
transactions versus list Green Thumb on the NASDAQ or New York Stock Exchange.
That's a fast path."* GTI intends to list "the business in full, not in part" and
has open dialogues with both major exchanges. The trigger is adult-use
rescheduling (see [2026-10-adult-use-rescheduling.md](2026-10-adult-use-rescheduling.md)).

**The direction of consolidation is GTI → RYM, not the reverse.** The CFO was
explicit: once GTI controls RYM, GAAP forces GTI to consolidate it; RYM
consolidating GTI "is just not possible from a GAAP accounting perspective." So
the end state is GTI uplisted on its own, with RYM absorbed underneath as a
consolidated brand/IP subsidiary — simpler to manage than a reverse merger into a
micro-cap shell.

**Control was taken ahead of rescheduling, not at it.** The 49.99% conversion cap
was removed effective 2026-10-10 (see below), so GTI's control of RYM no longer
waits on any federal catalyst.

**What RYM is for in the meantime:** a federally clean, Nasdaq-compliant home for
the brands, and the entity that can sell them **outside** state dispensaries. The
Oct 2026 Señorita license gives RYM exclusive rights to any non-dispensary channel
(e.g. hemp-derived THC beverages, "consistent with applicable Nasdaq listing
standards") while GTI's license is limited to the dispensary channel. Management
calls THC beverages one of the fastest-growing categories and tracks consumer
trends "particularly through our investment in RYTHM."

The vehicle was not designed from scratch. Agrify Corporation was a distressed
cannabis equipment company with an existing Nasdaq listing that GTI effectively
took over in late 2024 and renamed RYTHM Inc.

## Structure

RYTHM Inc. (Nasdaq: RYM, formerly Agrify Corporation, CIK 0001800637) is a
federally compliant, non-plant-touching IP holding company. GTI sold all brand IP
into RYM across two transactions in 2025, and added one license in 2026:

- **May 20, 2025** — incredibles brand IP acquired by RYM; GTI licenses it back
  via Incredibles License Agreement (revenue-based fee)
- **August 27, 2025** — RYTHM, Beboe, Dogwalkers, Doctor Solomon's, Shine, and
  Good Green IP sold to RYM; GTI licenses all back via fixed annual fees
  (amended from revenue-based to fixed, effective April 1, 2026)
- **October 13, 2026** — Señorita added to the August 2025 license for GTI's
  dispensary channel only; RYM keeps exclusive non-dispensary rights. Base fee
  +\$250K/yr. Done explicitly "in light of upcoming changes to federal law governing
  hemp-derived THC products" (see [2026-08-hemp-ban.md](2026-08-hemp-ban.md))

Ben Kovler chairs both GTI and RYM (and is RYM's interim CEO). GTI provides
management and operational support to RYM under shared services agreements.

## Convertible Notes — Three Tranches

All notes issued to RSLGH, LLC, an indirect wholly-owned GTI subsidiary.

| Tranche | Issued | Principal | Conversion price | Maturity |
|---------|--------|-----------|-----------------|----------|
| Nov 2024 | 2024-11-05 | undisclosed | **\$3.158 / share** | Nov 5, 2025 (amended) |
| May 2025 | 2025-05-22 | \$30M aggregate (GTI + co-investors) | **\$23.53 / share** | 2026-11-22 |
| Aug 2025 | 2025-08-25 | **\$50M** | **\$29.475 / share** | 2027-02-25 |

RSLGH's notes total **\$72.0M original principal** (per the Aug 2026 8-K).
Interest rate: 10% per annum on all tranches.
All notes convertible into common stock **or pre-funded warrants** at holder's
election. RSLGH also holds pre-funded warrants for up to **9,731,638 shares**.

Conversion prices are as originally reported; RYM had only **2,179,128 shares
outstanding** at 2026-08-10, so check whether a later reverse split rebased them.

## Control — Completed October 2026

1. **2026-08-10** — RYM special meeting approves share issuance to the note and
   warrant holders under Nasdaq Rule 5635 (1,118,058 for / 10,049 against; 51.8%
   of shares represented). Same day, RYM, RSLGH and VMS sign an amendment
   **removing all beneficial ownership limitations** on the notes, the warrants,
   and pre-funded warrants issuable under the shared services agreement.
2. **2026-08-11** — RYM 8-K Item 5.01 (change in control): RSLGH's beneficial
   ownership rises from **49.99% to \~89.9%** (Rule 13d-3 counts rights exercisable
   within 60 days). Aggregate consideration paid: **\$109.5M**, from GTI working
   capital plus note interest and services fees payable.
3. **2026-10-10** — Amendment effective; GTI begins consolidating RYM into its
   financial statements instead of equity-method accounting. Economics unchanged;
   reported lines change (licensing fees GTI pays RYM eliminate on consolidation).
4. **As of 2026-10-13** — GTI directly owns \~32% of RYM's outstanding common
   (698,961 of 2,179,128 shares at Aug 10), with the right to go to \~90%. The notes
   and warrants had not yet been converted.

The old route — convert to 49.99%, buy the last 0.02% on market — is moot. The
cap is gone.

## What We Don't Know

- **Whether and when GTI actually converts.** Control is via exercisable rights;
  the May 2025 tranche matures 2026-11-22, so conversion, extension or repayment
  of that tranche is the next visible step
- **Principal amount of the Nov 2024 note** — not disclosed in its 8-K; check
  RYM's 10-K financial statement notes or the EX-4.1 exhibit in the Nov 2024 filing
- **Fixed annual licensing fees** — not public; on GTI's side they showed up as
  gross margin compression (\$17.5M in Q2-2026), which should eliminate on
  consolidation from Q4-2026
- **RYM's Nasdaq standing under a plant-touching parent** — the Señorita terms are
  written to stay "consistent with applicable Nasdaq listing standards," but how
  Nasdaq treats a \~90%-controlled subsidiary of a Schedule I operator is unverified
- **How RYM is folded into GTI after uplisting** — management has not said whether
  minority RYM holders are bought out, merged in, or left in place
