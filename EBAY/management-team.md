# eBay Inc. (NASDAQ: EBAY) — Management Team

## Executive Leadership

---

### Jamie Iannone — President & Chief Executive Officer

**Background:**
- Career built across e-commerce, consumer devices, and membership retail. Equally comfortable on the product/tech side and on operations
- Earlier management-consulting background before moving into e-commerce operating roles

**Career prior to CEO role:**
- **eBay (first stint, \~2001–2009):** senior product and buyer-experience roles in the marketplace's peak-growth years. He knew the platform and the culture before he came back as CEO
- **Barnes & Noble (\~2009–2014):** led the digital/Nook devices and digital content business
- **Walmart / Sam's Club (\~2014–2020):** ran SamsClub.com and Sam's Club's membership & technology functions, then became **COO of Walmart eCommerce**
- Named eBay CEO effective **April 2020**, after the 2019 activist campaign (Elliott, Starboard) and the departure of Devin Wenig

**Role and track record at eBay:**
- Finished the portfolio simplification the activists pushed for: StubHub sale (2020), Classifieds to Adevinta (2021), Korea to Emart (2021), and later the monetization of the Adevinta stake
- Repositioned eBay from "everything store" toward enthusiast categories. His "Focus Categories" strategy (collectibles, motors P&A, luxury, refurbished) went from \~2021 pilots to >40% of GMV growing 26% (Q2-2026)
- Drove the managed-payments, advertising (\~\$2B/yr), and shipping layers that lift the take rate
- Bolt-on M&A in the core enthusiast niches: TCGplayer, Goldin, Caramel, Tise, and **Depop** (\$1.4B, closed July 2026)
- Under him, growth re-accelerated from \~flat (2022–24) to double-digit GMV and revenue growth in late 2025–2026. On the Q4-2025 call he said "the underlying health of the business is the strongest it's been since I joined the company six years ago"
- Announced restructurings in Jan 2024 (\~1,000 roles) and Oct 2026 (\~9% of the global workforce)

### Peggy Alford — Senior Vice President & Chief Financial Officer

**Background:**
- 20+ years in finance and operations at technology and payments companies
- Has sat on the **Meta Platforms (Facebook) board of directors** since 2019

**Career prior to eBay CFO role:**
- **eBay / PayPal (earlier stints):** finance roles within the eBay family, including Rent.com (an eBay subsidiary) and senior finance/operations positions at PayPal. Her 2025 appointment release described it as a "return" to eBay
- **Chan Zuckerberg Initiative:** CFO and head of operations
- **PayPal:** senior executive roles, most recently leading **global sales**

**Role:**
- Appointed CFO effective **May 12, 2025**, succeeding Steve Priest (announced Apr 30, 2025 with Q1-2025 results)
- Owns guidance. The cadence: next-quarter revenue/GMV/EPS ranges every quarter, a numerical full-year revenue range only at the Q3 report, and qualitative full-year framing otherwise ("revenue growth in line to slightly ahead of GMV"). At Q2-2026 she guided FY2026 revenue growth of 11–12% FX-neutral, including Depop
- Oversaw adoption of ASU 2025-06 (internal-use software) from Jan 1, 2026 under the full retrospective method. All 2026 YoY growth rates are quoted against recast 2025 figures

### Jordan Sweetnam — Chief Commercial Officer

**Background and role:**
- Long-time eBay market/category leader who previously ran the **North America** marketplace business
- In the May 2025 reorganization he took over the newly combined **Global Markets and Product** organization as Chief Commercial Officer. That put product and market (P&L) teams under one leader, "designed to enhance speed, alignment, and customer-centricity"
- The reorg came with the departure of Chief Product Officer Eddie Garcia

---

### Mazen Rawashdeh — Chief Technology Officer

**Background and role:**
- Senior engineering leader. Before eBay he ran large-scale infrastructure and engineering at consumer internet companies
- Since the May 2025 reorganization he leads eBay's **unified engineering organization** as CTO. Engineering was consolidated into one org "to operate with greater speed and drive operational scale"
- Responsible for the AI roadmap the company now leans on in its narrative: Magical Listing, AI card scanning (80M+ scans), agentic search, and automated vehicle title/VIN verification

### Steve Priest — Former Chief Financial Officer (2020–May 2025)

- Joined eBay as CFO in 2020, early in the Iannone era. Previously CFO at Ancestry.com
- Steered the company through the pandemic demand spike and hangover and through the divestiture/capital-return program
- Left as CFO on May 11, 2025 and stayed on in an advisory role until July 31, 2025

## Corporate Governance Notes

- **Single share class:** one class of common stock, one vote per share, Nasdaq-listed. No founder control. Pierre Omidyar (founder) has no operating role
- **Independent chair:** the CEO and chair roles are separate. The board is chaired by an independent director (Paul Pressler, former Gap Inc. CEO). It grew to **12 members** in March 2026 with the appointment of Brian Sharples (co-founder of HomeAway)
- **Activist legacy:** Elliott Management and Starboard Value pushed in 2019 for the StubHub and Classifieds sales and for capital returns. The current "core marketplace + buybacks" model came out of that campaign
- **Capital-return discipline:** \~\$3B a year returned recently (FY2025: \$2.5B buybacks plus \$531M dividends; quarterly dividend \$0.31 in 2026). Shares outstanding fell \~17% from Q1-2023 to Q2-2026
- **2019 cyberstalking scandal:** former eBay security employees harassed the editors of a newsletter critical of the company. Several were criminally convicted. In January 2024 eBay entered a deferred prosecution agreement with the US DOJ and paid a \$3M penalty, which included an independent compliance monitor
- **Restructurings:** Jan 2024 (\~1,000 roles, \~9%) and Oct 20, 2026 (\~9% of the global workforce, item 2.05 8-K). The Oct 2026 8-K also pre-announced Q3-2026 results **above** the August guidance ranges
- **Accounting note:** the Q1-2025 10-Q was amended (10-Q/A filed May 9, 2025). ASU 2025-06 was adopted on a full retrospective basis in 2026
