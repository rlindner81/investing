# eBay Inc. (NASDAQ: EBAY) — Business Plan

## Company Overview

eBay Inc. runs one of the world's largest online marketplaces, connecting buyers and sellers in more than 190 markets. Unlike first-party retailers, eBay holds no inventory. It earns a take rate on the gross merchandise volume (GMV) that moves across its platform. Roughly 90% of GMV is non-new or non-seasonal goods: collectibles, pre-owned, refurbished, parts, luxury. After a decade of low growth, management has refocused the company on "Focus Categories" (collectibles/trading cards, motors parts & accessories, luxury/fashion, refurbished), consumer-to-consumer (C2C) selling, and recommerce. Since late 2025 that strategy has re-accelerated growth to double digits.

- **Founded:** September 1995 by Pierre Omidyar as AuctionWeb, San Jose, California
- **IPO:** September 1998 (Nasdaq: EBAY)
- **Headquarters:** San Jose, California
- **FY End:** December 31 (calendar year)
- **FY2025 Revenue:** \$11,100M (+7.9% YoY); GMV \~\$79.6B (+\~6% FX-neutral)
- **Run-rate (Q2-2026):** revenue \$3,134M (+14.8% YoY), GMV \$22.4B (+14% FX-neutral)

**Portfolio history.** eBay has spent ten years slimming down to the core marketplace. PayPal was spun off in July 2015. StubHub was sold to viagogo (2020). Classifieds went to Adevinta (2021) for cash plus an equity stake that has since been monetized. A majority of the Korea business went to Emart (2021). Since then it has made bolt-on deals in its Focus Categories: TCGplayer (trading-card marketplace), Goldin (high-end collectibles auctions), Caramel (vehicle transaction tooling), Tise (Nordic C2C fashion, closed Oct 1, 2025), and **Depop** (C2C fashion, bought from Etsy for \~\$1.2B headline / \$1.4B paid incl. adjustments, closed July 30, 2026).

## Revenue Model

eBay's revenue is GMV × take rate. The take rate was \~14% in Q2-2026, flat YoY.

**Transaction revenue (the bulk):**
- Final value fees (percentage of sale price plus shipping), per-order fees, listing/insertion fees, and store subscriptions
- **Managed payments** (fully in-house since \~2021, Adyen as processor). eBay intermediates checkout, which lets it monetize payments, fight fraud, and offer financial services (Klarna/BNPL, seller working capital, eBay Balance, Pay by Bank in the UK)
- **Shipping programs** (eBay International Shipping, UK Managed Shipping, domestic label programs). Shipping revenue grew double digits in Q2-2026

**Advertising (\~\$2B annual run-rate in FY2025):**
- **First-party ads:** Promoted Listings (CPS "general" and CPC "priority/advanced" formats) and Promoted Stores. Q2-2026 first-party ads were \$570M (+24% YoY); total ads were \$596M, or \~2.7% of GMV
- About 1.3B of eBay's 2.6B live listings are promoted, and 5.7M sellers used at least one promoted-listing product in Q2-2026
- **Off-platform ads** (+21% in Q2-2026), mainly through the Qoo10 business in Japan

**Monetization pattern:** management guides revenue growth "in line to slightly ahead of" GMV. Advertising, shipping, and financial services lift the take rate. Mix shift toward lower-take-rate categories (vehicles, bullion, eBay Live) offsets that.

## Products & Platforms

### eBay Marketplace (core)
- Auction and fixed-price ("Buy It Now") listings across \~2.6B live listings
- \~136M trailing-twelve-month active buyers (Q2-2026) and \~16M "enthusiast" buyers, who spend about \$3,600/year each (TTM)
- **Authenticity Guarantee** covers sneakers, watches, handbags, jewelry, trading cards (US and UK as of Q2-2026), and 100+ apparel brands
- **Magical Listing:** an AI-native listing flow that builds a full listing from a photo, aimed at unlocking C2C supply

### Focus Categories (>40% of GMV in Q2-2026, +26% YoY)
- **Collectibles:** trading cards (sports, Pokémon, One Piece, Magic), coins and bullion, comics, toys, memorabilia. Supported by an AI card scanner (80M+ scans) and Card Ladder price indexes
- **eBay Motors:** parts & accessories (Guaranteed Fit, now in five markets) and **vehicles**, where native vehicle listing and AI title/VIN verification have cut average pickup time from 2+ weeks to \~4 days
- **Fashion / Luxury:** pre-loved apparel, handbags, watches, jewelry
- **Refurbished:** eBay Refurbished program with graded condition standards

### Off-platform marketplaces
- **TCGplayer:** trading-card marketplace and seller tools, including Roca sorting machines for hobby shops
- **Goldin:** premium collectibles auction house (record sales of sports cards and memorabilia in 2026)
- **Depop:** Gen Z C2C fashion resale app, consolidated from Q3-2026
- **Tise:** Nordic C2C fashion

### Emerging growth vectors
- **eBay Live:** live-stream shopping, mainly collectibles. Growing at a \~7x YoY run-rate in early 2026, expanded to Germany, France, Italy, and Canada
- **Agentic commerce:** natural-language/agentic search pilots and integrations with third-party AI assistants

## Technology & Competitive Position

### Unique, non-commoditized inventory
About 90% of GMV is used, refurbished, collectible, or otherwise one-of-a-kind. Condition, scarcity, and pricing judgment matter for these goods, which makes eBay harder to displace by Amazon-style first-party retail or low-price cross-border apps (Temu, Shein). It is also arguably harder to disintermediate by AI shopping agents.

### Trust infrastructure
Seller feedback, the eBay Money Back Guarantee, Authenticity Guarantee, Guaranteed Fit, and post-transaction protections are built on 30 years of transaction data. Management calls this a structural advantage that is hard to replicate.

### Two-sided network in enthusiast niches
eBay is the default liquidity venue for trading cards and many collectibles. With TCGplayer and Goldin it now spans the collectibles value chain from everyday cards to premium auctions.

### Capital-light model
eBay holds no inventory and runs a \~74% non-GAAP gross margin with \~28% non-GAAP operating margin. Capex is light (\~\$0.5B/yr), so free cash flow runs at \~\$1.5–2.0B a year. Nearly all of it goes back to shareholders.

## Markets & Channel Mix

- **GMV split is roughly 50/50 US vs. international** (Q4-2025: US \$10.7B, international \$10.5B)
- **US** is the growth engine: GMV +19% in Q4-2025 and +24% in Q2-2026, driven by collectibles, luxury, Live, vehicles, and the Klarna partnership
- **International** (UK, Germany, Australia, Canada, cross-border trade from Greater China) has lagged on European macro weakness and US trade-policy changes. The removal of the US de minimis exemption in Aug 2025 hurt cross-border volume. International GMV returned to +4% in Q2-2026
- **Mix:** C2C is \~25% of GMV, and recommerce (pre-owned plus refurbished) was over 40% of eBay-platform GMV in 2025

## Growth Strategy

1. **Focus Categories:** category-specific trust, tools, and marketing in collectibles, motors, fashion, and refurbished. These grew 26% in Q2-2026
2. **C2C revival:** free selling for private sellers in the UK and Germany, Magical Listing, and the Depop and Tise acquisitions
3. **Recommerce / circular economy:** brand partnerships and certified recycled auto parts
4. **Monetization layers:** higher ad penetration, shipping programs, and financial services (BNPL, eBay Balance, Pay by Bank)
5. **Emerging vectors:** eBay Live, vehicles, agentic commerce
6. **Capital returns:** \~\$3B returned in FY2025 (\$2.5B buybacks plus \$531M dividends; dividend \$0.31/quarter in 2026). Share count fell from 536M (Q1-2023) to 445M (Q2-2026), about −17%
7. **Efficiency:** on Oct 20, 2026 eBay announced a restructuring that cuts \~9% of its global workforce. In the same 8-K it said Q3-2026 results would come in **above** the guidance ranges issued in August

## Key Risks

### Durability of the collectibles boom
A large share of the 2025–26 acceleration comes from trading cards, bullion, and coins, which are cyclical and speculative. Management itself calls the bullion/coin tailwinds "less durable." A cooling hobby cycle would hit growth and the US mix.

### Take-rate dilution
Vehicles, bullion, Live, and high-ASP items monetize below the marketplace average. Revenue growth depends on ads, shipping, and payments outrunning this mix drag.

### International weakness and trade policy
Half of GMV is outside the US. European consumer weakness, tariffs, and the end of de minimis hit cross-border trade, especially Greater China → US.

### Competition
Competitors include Amazon, Temu/Shein/TikTok Shop, Whatnot (live collectibles), Fanatics/Collectors (cards), Vinted (European C2C fashion), and Facebook Marketplace. AI shopping agents may also try to sit between buyers and eBay.

### Integration risk
Depop (\$1.4B) is eBay's largest acquisition in years. Integrating a Gen Z brand without losing its culture is a recurring problem for incumbents.

### Leverage and accounting changes
Gross debt is \~\$6.7B against \~\$2.3B cash (plus investments), and eBay uses commercial paper. Adopting ASU 2025-06 (internal-use software) in 2026 restated prior-period OCF/capex and operating expense, so YoY comparisons in 2026 are against recast figures.
