# Bending Spoons S.p.A. (Nasdaq: BSP) — Management Team

## Executive Leadership

Bending Spoons is still run by its founding team. Three of the four class-A-holding co-founders (Ferrari, Patarnello, Danieli) met at the Technical University of Denmark and the University of Padua and co-founded the failed diary-app startup Evertale (2010–13). The fourth, Luca Querella, was an Evertale standout who joined at BSP's founding in 2013. Every other executive was promoted from within; none was hired from outside as a senior leader. The team is very young: the median executive age is in the mid-30s.

Address for all officers: Via Nino Bonnet 10, 20154 Milan, Italy.

## Corporate Governance Notes

- **Dual-class control:** Class A shares (5 votes each, held only by the four co-founders: 310.2M shares) carry \~83% of voting power post-IPO, and ordinary shares carry 1 vote each. Class A converts 1:1 into ordinary shares on request and automatically on certain transfers. In practice, outside shareholders cannot change control.
- **Italian S.p.A.:** governed by the Italian Civil Code under the "board of directors + audit committee" system (*comitato per il controllo sulla gestione*), adopted April 23, 2026. At least one third of directors must be independent; 5 of 9 are.
- **Foreign private issuer:** quarterly results come voluntarily on 6-K, with an annual 20-F. FPIs are exempt from the proxy rules and Reg FD, and insiders file Forms 3/4 only voluntarily (BSP insiders did file Forms 4 in September 2026).
- **Major outside holders (pre-IPO):** Baillie Gifford (15.0% of ordinary shares), Galileo Quattordici (11.6%, part-seller), Cox Investment Holdings (9.3%), Durable Capital (6.7%), StarTIP / Tamburi (6.2%), and Berkshire Hathaway (\$600M at IPO plus \$330M in Oct 2026, \~4%).
- **Lock-up:** 180 days from the June 30, 2026 prospectus covers the company, officers, directors, selling shareholders, and "substantially all" holders. It expires around **late December 2026**.
- **No dividends:** all cash is retained for acquisitions.
- **Compensation:** equity compensation was \$55.5M in 2025, and the IPO triggered vesting of one-off awards (\$11M expense in Q3-2026). Management says it has "plenty of room" to raise pay to attract talent.
