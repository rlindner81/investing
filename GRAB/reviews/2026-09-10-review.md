**Revenue trend.** Growth is intact and mildly re-accelerating versus FY-2024: revenue rose +21.7% Y/Y in q2-2026 to \$997M and +23.5% in q1-2026, against +20.5% for full-year 2025 and +17.0% for FY-2024. TTM revenue is \$3.73B. Four consecutive quarters above +18% Y/Y at this scale is a genuinely strong top line, and it is the single best thing in this table.

**FCF profile — the problem.** Cash generation has deteriorated sharply and this is not a one-quarter artifact. FY-2024 produced \$775M of company FCF (a +27.7% margin); FY-2025 produced **negative** \$18M; TTM now sits at **−\$160M**. Operating cash flow itself has turned: −\$60M TTM against +\$852M in FY-2024. The strict ex-SBC line is materially worse — **−\$426M** TTM, an −11.4% margin — because SBC runs \~\$240M/yr and capex has climbed (\$100M PP&E + \$26M software TTM vs. \$77M + \$36M in FY-2024). The gap between the two FCF flavours is \~\$266M, essentially all SBC, and that is real dilution being excluded from the company-preferred number. q3-2025 was the worst single quarter (−\$154M company, −\$214M strict). Both P/FCF columns print `n/m` for TTM and every recent quarter — there is no positive FCF base to value against.

**Valuation.** TTM P/S has compressed hard to **3.3x**, down from 7.0–7.3x through FY-2024 and 5.1x at FY-2025. Forward P/S on FY guidance is **3.0x**. For \~22% revenue growth that is not expensive on a sales basis — the multiple has de-rated roughly in half while growth held. The catch is that P/S is the only multiple that works here, because FCF is negative; you are paying 3x sales for a business that currently consumes cash.

**Guidance trajectory.** Credible and rising. FY-2026 guidance was raised at q2 to \$4,100–4,150M from \$4,040–4,100M. History shows delivery: FY actual came in +0.2% to +2.6% above guide in most prior periods, with only q3-2025 slightly light (−0.6%).

**Balance sheet.** \$2.86B cash against \$2.03B debt = **\~\$0.83B net cash**, roughly 7% of the \$12.4B market cap. Note debt jumped from \~\$385M (q1-2025) to \~\$2.0B, so the net cash cushion is thinner than it once was. SBC at \$240M/yr against negative FCF is the burden that matters most.

**Verdict.** Cheap on sales and growing above 20% with raised guidance, but the cash-flow collapse from +\$775M to −\$160M is not yet explained by the multiple alone — a de-rated grower with deteriorating FCF, not an obvious bargain.

## 3. News

**The surface narrative.** Three threads dominate the last 2–4 weeks: insider selling, a \$750M buyback, and an unexplained volume shock. The financial-media framing — "GRAB dips on CEO share sale" — attributes the September slide to insider activity. That framing does not survive the arithmetic.

**Insider sales — substantiated, but far too small to be the cause.** These are real and confirmed in the SEC record: the company's filing history for Aug–Sep 2026 consists almost entirely of Form 4s and Form 144s. CEO Anthony Tan sold 400,000 shares for \~\$1.45M, leaving his *direct* Class A stake at 428,498 shares; President/COO Alexander Hungate sold \~145,000 Class A shares on 2026-09-02 at \$3.48 under a Rule 10b5-1 plan (\~\$506K). Two caveats matter. First, magnitude: \~\$2M of combined selling cannot explain 150M shares (\~\$460M) of turnover — the causal story is \~200x too small. Second, Tan's economic control runs through Class B super-voting shares, so the "direct Class A stake" figure overstates how much he actually stepped back. A 10b5-1 plan sale is also pre-scheduled and carries little signaling value. **Verdict: substantiated as fact, mostly attention as an explanation.**

**\$750M buyback and raised guidance — substantiated, and positive.** Announced 2026-08-04 with Q2: revenue +22% Y/Y to \$997M, adjusted EBITDA +54% to \$168M, on-demand GMV +21% to \$6.5B, MTUs at a record 53.9M, FY-2026 revenue guidance raised to \$4.10–4.15B. All confirmed in the 6-K. Two honest qualifiers: net profit of \$235M was flattered by a **\$307M one-off gain** from consolidating Superbank, so underlying profitability is weaker than the headline; and an authorization is not a commitment. Grab has, however, actually executed — \$351M repurchased under the prior \$500M program by 2026-07-31 via ASR/forward agreements. Gross cash liquidity \$7.4B. **Substantiated.**

**Indonesia 8% commission cap — substantiated, already in effect.** Presidential Regulation 27/2026 caps two-wheel ride-hailing commissions at 8%, effective 2026-07-01. Grab guided that Indonesian ojol is under 6% of Mobility GMV with margins holding in historical bands. Real, but old news and bounded.

**The September 9 move — no disclosed cause.** This is the important finding and I want to state it precisely: GRAB fell 6.46% to \$3.04 on \~148–150M shares, \~3.3x average, while SPY fell only 0.48%. I checked for a company release, registered secondary, F-3/424B, or 13D/G — **there is none**; the SEC record shows no 6-K after 2026-08-13. No index deletion tied to GRAB surfaced (the September MSCI/FTSE Indonesia changes name GOTO, a competitor, not GRAB). So I cannot identify the driver, and I would rather say that than name a culprit. Turnover of that size on no news usually means a large holder repositioning off-exchange or a systematic/forced unwind, but that is an inference, not a fact — treat the cause as unknown pending the next 13F/13D cycle.

**Sentiment and catalysts.** Sell-side remains bullish with steadily *falling* targets — BofA \$4.90 (from \$5.20), Barclays \$5 (from \$7), Morgan Stanley \$6.25 — a pattern of ratings held while targets chase price down, which is weak evidence. Short interest is 6.9% and fell 25%. Uber retains \~13% and its economic interest was explicitly unchanged by Khosrowshahi's July 6 board exit — but that stake is a persistent overhang. Watch: Q3-2026 earnings (early November), and the Taiwan FTC decision on the Foodpanda Taiwan acquisition, extended to **October 27**.

**Risks.** An unexplained \~\$460M distribution day at 52-week lows; regulatory precedent in Indonesia inviting replication; Uber's overhang; and profitability that leans on a one-off gain.

**Rating:** -0.6 — SELL

## Verdict

GRAB is a \~22%-grower that has de-rated to 3.3x TTM sales (3.0x forward) with raised guidance, a credible delivery record against guide, \~\$0.83B net cash and a \$750M buyback the company is actually executing — the setup that usually makes a stock interesting. Two things stop it from being one yet. First, cash generation has genuinely broken down: company FCF went from +\$775M in FY-2024 to −\$160M TTM, and −\$426M once \~\$240M/yr of SBC and all capitalized spend are stripped out, so the cheap-looking P/S is the only multiple that even computes. Second, the tape is in unambiguous distribution — 52-week lows, stacked bearish SMAs against benchmarks that are cleanly bullish, and a 150M-share day on 2026-09-09 with **no disclosed cause** that I could find in the SEC record or the press. The main risk is that the volume shock reflects a large holder exiting for a reason not yet public, in which case price leads the fundamentals. I would want either the cause of that day identified (next 13F/13D cycle) or a return to positive operating cash flow at Q3 in early November before treating the valuation as support rather than a falling knife; conversely, evidence that FY-2025/2026 cash burn was deliberate investment with FCF inflecting back positive would change this materially.

**Rating:** -0.7 — SELL
