**Revenue trend.** The decline is narrowing sharply: Q3-2026 revenue of \$145.0M was -2.0% Y/Y after -26.2% (Q1) and -25.1% (Q2), and landed 3.2% above the \$141M midpoint guided in September. Read the Q4 guide carefully, though. \$136–137M (-10% to -11% Y/Y) means the decline *re-widens* by 8–9 points against a holiday comp of \$152.7M. The FY raise from \$656M to \$660M is almost exactly the Q3 beat (\~\$4.5M) carried through, so Q4 is unchanged. Repeat revenue (>80% of the mix) has stabilized. First-order revenue is still down high-teens, and that is the IL MAKIAGE problem.

**FCF profile.** Company FCF was \$10.0M in Q3 and \$13.6M in Q2, but TTM is still -\$3.4M (-0.5% margin) because Q4-2025 and Q1-2026 were negative. The strict ex-SBC figure is much worse at -\$53.1M TTM (-7.9%). Capitalized software has risen to \~\$15.6M a year from \~\$5M, and SBC of \$34.1M equals roughly 5% of sales, more than the business currently generates in cash. FY adjusted EBITDA guidance of \$32–34M is a \~5% margin, and the Q4 guide of \$4–6M implies \~3–4%.

**Valuation.** At \$19.90 the market cap is \~\$926M, or 1.4x TTM and forward sales. P/FCF is n/m on both measures. The table's cash row (\$168M) shows cash only. Adding \$392M of marketable securities gives \$561M of liquidity against the \~\$550M face (\$538M carrying) zero-coupon 2030 exchangeable, so the balance sheet is about net-neutral and EV is roughly the market cap. On that basis EV/FY26 EBITDA is \~27x. On Q3's annualized \$88M run-rate it is \~10x. The stock is cheap on sales and expensive on trough earnings. Which one matters depends on whether IL MAKIAGE margins come back.

**Guidance track record.** ODD has beaten every next-quarter revenue guide in the window by 1.5–3.9%, and the FY raise trajectory has resumed (\$656M → \$660M). The beats are consistent and small, which reads as conservative guiding rather than upside surprise.

**Capital.** The share count is down \~19% since Q4-2025 (57.6M → 46.5M) after \$163M of buybacks, and \$86M of authorization remains. Short interest fell to 10.7% of shares outstanding from 17.4% at Q2.

**Verdict:** Cheap on sales with a protected balance sheet, but there is no FCF support, and the Q4 guide says the recovery is not yet linear.

**Rating:** 0.4 — HOLD

## 3. News

**The surface narrative.** One story dominates: Q3-2026, released pre-open on Nov 11. Headlines read "results ahead of plan, raises full-year outlook." Revenue was \$145.0M (-2% Y/Y vs. a \~-5% guide and \~\$140.1M consensus), adjusted EBITDA \$22M (vs. \$18–20M), and adjusted EPS \$0.37 (vs. \~\$0.30). FY guidance moved to \~-18.5% revenue and \$32–34M adjusted EBITDA (from -19% and \$30–32M). Coverage pairs the beat with a Goldman Sachs Sell at a \$9 target. Goldman calls a return to 20% growth / 20% EBITDA margins "elusive for the foreseeable future."

**The deeper read.** The beat is **substantiated**. It comes from the company's own GlobeNewswire release (the 6-K is not yet on EDGAR). FCF ties out (\$10M Q3, \$2.5M 9M), as do the \$561M of cash plus investments and the undrawn \$350M facility. The "raise" deserves less credit than the headline gives it. The FY revenue increase (\~\$656M → \~\$660M) only flows through the Q3 beat, and Q4 is still guided at -10% to -11% with \$4–6M EBITDA. Analysts noted on the call that the Q4 guide was unchanged. Gross margin fell to 67.4% from 71.6%, below expectations, on lower AOV.

The IL MAKIAGE recovery remains **mostly narrative**. Holtzman still says "we are not there yet" and "remain very hopeful." His only concrete CPA evidence cuts both ways: September was "the best CPA since 2025," then "October was not that good." He also confirmed that the unnamed ad partner drives "way more" than 50% of the category, which is a sharper statement of concentration than before. The partner, still never named and presumed to be Meta, has made no public statement. The ex-IL MAKIAGE engine *is* substantiated: SpoiledChild is tracking to \>35% growth and \~\$350M, and METHODIQ is ahead of SpoiledChild's first year.

**METHODIQ peptides.** The HHS enforcement-discretion policy for six compounded peptides (BPC-157, TB-500 and others) is **real**: it was filed Oct 28 and published in the Federal Register Oct 30. It is also explicitly interim, non-binding and revocable, and state enforcement is unresolved. ODD's 2027 injectables/peptides plan (NAD+, semaglutide and the six peptides) is a launch plan with no committed revenue. That makes it optionality, not data.

**Price moves.** The -9.2% drop on Nov 10, the session before the print, has no identifiable news driver. Positioning ahead of earnings is the likeliest explanation. The \~+2% close on earnings day, against +21.8% after Q2, says the market had already priced the beat.

**Catalysts.** The 6-K filing (to confirm the figures), Q4/FY-2026 results around late February 2027 (first 2027 guide, with "return to growth" the stated goal), Brand 4 and the METHODIQ longevity/metabolic launch in 2027, and remaining buybacks (\$86M).

**Risks.** Single-ad-partner dependence, a Q4 re-deceleration, gross-margin erosion, \$158M of inventory (IL MAKIAGE heavy), regulatory risk on compounded peptides and GLP-1s, and Israel exposure.

## Verdict

Q3-2026 confirmed that ODDITY's floor is real. Repeat revenue has stabilized, SpoiledChild and METHODIQ are compounding, the decline narrowed to -2%, and \~\$561M of liquidity against \~\$550M of exchangeable notes leaves the \~\$926M market cap at a modest 1.4x sales. The recovery is still not linear or proven: Q4 is guided back to -10% to -11% with only \$4–6M of EBITDA, the FY "raise" is just the Q3 beat, TTM free cash flow is negative, and IL MAKIAGE still depends on an ad partner that drives well over half of category demand and has not acknowledged a fix. The market treated the beat as priced in. The stock faded on heavy volume into a short-term downtrend and has overhead supply at \~\$20.5–21.6. A Q4 print at or above the guide, a 2027 outlook that commits to growth, or sustained IL MAKIAGE CPA improvement would justify a BUY. A Q4 slip, further gross-margin erosion, or a break below the 50-day toward the \~\$16.6 volume shelf would move this to SELL.
