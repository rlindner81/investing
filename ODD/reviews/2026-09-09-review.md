Q2-2026 is the first genuine inflection in a brutal downcycle, but the multiple no longer prices distress. Revenue of \$180.5M fell 25.1% Y/Y, marginally better than Q1's -26.2% and landing at the favorable end of the -25%/-30% guide. That is the second consecutive quarter of \~25% declines against a FY2025 that grew 25.2% to \$809.8M — a violent swing driven almost entirely by the IL MAKIAGE ad-account dislocation. TTM revenue has compressed to \$679.1M.

The cash-flow reversal is the quarter's most important datapoint. Operating CF swung to +\$14.3M from -\$20.2M in Q1, and company FCF turned positive at +\$13.6M (a +7.5% margin) versus -\$21.1M. On the strict ex-SBC basis, though, FCF is only breakeven (-\$0.1M): \$9.8M of quarterly SBC and \$3.9M of capitalized software absorb essentially all of the reported free cash flow. TTM remains deeply negative on both flavours (-\$23.3M company, -\$74.9M strict), so P/FCF is n/m and the FCF recovery is one quarter old, not a trend.

Valuation is 1.1x TTM P/S and 1.1x forward on both the reinstated FY guide and the Q3 guide — against 5.7x as recently as Q2-2025 and 3.9x for FY2024. That is cheap in absolute terms for a brand portfolio still printing 68.7% gross margins, but it is a fair price for a business whose top line is shrinking and whose recovery depends on a third party. Balance sheet is the real support: \$167M cash plus \$394M marketable securities (\$561M total) against \$537M of exchangeable notes — roughly net-flat, improved by retiring \$50M of face value for \$35M. Management repurchased 11.7M shares for \$163M YTD, cutting the count \~20% to 45.9M, which mechanically lifts per-share value if revenue stabilizes.

Guidance credibility is intact — the company beat every NQ guide by 1.5–3.9% historically and reinstated FY guidance (\~-19%, \~\$656M) after withdrawing it twice. Cheap on sales, unproven on cash.

**Rating:** 0.6 — BUY

**The surface narrative.** Q2-2026 (reported pre-market Sept 9) is the only story: revenue -25% to \$180.5M, adjusted EPS \$0.20 versus \$0.16 consensus, adjusted EBITDA \$13M against an \$8–10M guide, and Q3 guided to just -5% Y/Y — a \~20-point sequential improvement. The recovery thesis, however, is **substantially less substantiated than the price move implies**. The entire bull case rests on the IL MAKIAGE "algorithm dislocation" being technical and solvable. Critically, the ad partner — universally assumed to be Meta but **never named by ODDITY** — has made no public statement acknowledging any problem, and there is no third-party confirmation that a fix is underway. What *is* hard data underneath the guide: repeat revenue >100% 12-month net repeat rate, SpoiledChild tracking to \~\$350M (+35%), METHODIQ ahead of SpoiledChild's first year, and the company shifting 50%+ of acquisition from "try" to "buy" without unit-economics damage. Risks: single-ad-partner concentration is an unresolved binary, excess IL MAKIAGE inventory persists into 2027, gross margin has shed 360bps, and Israel exposure remains volatile. ## Verdict

Q2-2026 delivered the first real evidence that ODDITY's decline is bottoming: revenue at the good end of guidance, EBITDA above it, operating cash flow back positive, and a Q3 guide implying a 20-point sequential improvement in the rate of decline. At 1.1x sales with \$561M of cash and investments and \~20% of shares retired this year, the downside is genuinely cushioned, and the non-IL MAKIAGE portfolio (SpoiledChild toward \$350M, METHODIQ ahead of plan) is compounding independently of the problem. The main risk is that the whole recovery hinges on an ad-platform issue ODDITY cannot fix unilaterally — the partner has never publicly acknowledged it, management still says "we are not there yet," and TTM free cash flow is negative on any strict measure. A +22% move on 12x normal volume, with 26% of the float short, also front-runs proof that has not arrived. Confirmation of IL MAKIAGE normalization or a Q3 print at/above the -5% guide would justify a materially higher rating; a Q4 that slips back toward double-digit declines, or evidence the dislocation is structural rather than technical, would break the thesis outright.
