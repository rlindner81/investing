## Workday to Acquire C1.ai, a Leading Agentic Identity Platform for People and Agents

**URL:** https://newsroom.workday.com/2026-10-13-Workday-to-Acquire-C1-ai,-a-Leading-Agentic-Identity-Platform-for-People-and-Agents

* [Download PDF](https://newsroom.workday.com/2026-10-13-Workday-to-Acquire-C1-ai%2C-a-Leading-Agentic-Identity-Platform-for-People-and-Agents?asPDF=1 "Download PDF")

*With C1.ai, Workday Can Give Every Person and AI Agent the Right Access from the Day They Join to the Day They Leave — and Everywhere in Between*

LAS VEGAS, Oct. 13, 2026 /[PRNewswire](http://www.prnewswire.com/)/ -- **Workday Rising 2026** – [Workday, Inc.](https://www.workday.com/en-us/homepage.html) (NASDAQ: WDAY), the trusted agentic platform for HR, finance, and IT, today announced it has signed a definitive agreement to acquire [C1.ai](https://www.c1.ai/), a leading agentic identity platform for people and agents. C1.ai connects to the systems a business runs on – from email and CRM to code repositories, finance systems, and cloud infrastructure – giving each person and agent the right access at the right time and removing it the moment it's no longer needed.

![Workday](https://mmx.prnewswire.com/media/MS1321030/Workday-Logo-1.jpg?id=OA3014724 "Workday")

Most organizations manage employee access across hundreds of applications through manual service tickets, spreadsheets, and periodic reviews as workers join, move, and leave. The rapid adoption of AI agents has accelerated this challenge: C1.ai's [research](https://www.c1.ai/news/press-release/future-of-identity-security-2026) shows that 95% of large U.S. companies already run autonomous agents, yet according to [Deloitte's State of AI research](https://www.deloitte.com/content/dam/assets-zone3/us/en/docs/services/consulting/2026/state-of-ai-2026.pdf), only 21% report having a mature way to govern them. Organizations need to know who and what has access, why they have it, and when to remove it. Without those controls, AI agents can become lawless, creating security and compliance risks across the business.

Every new hire, job change, and exit is a Workday event. Workday already governs the people, money, and agents at the heart of the enterprise. With C1.ai, Workday will become the identity governance layer for people, money, and agents. Every employee and agent using Workday can have the right access to the apps, data, and systems they need, from the day they join to the day they leave, and every move in between.

"Today's companies run on a combined workforce of people and AI agents," said Gerrit Kazmaier, president, product and technology, Workday. "With C1.ai, we'll help secure that workforce by managing and enforcing exactly who – and what – has access to connected apps and systems, giving each person and agent the right access at the right moment to make work easier, safer, and more compliant."

"Enterprises can't secure the AI era using yesterday's IT tickets," said Alex Bovee, CEO and co-founder, C1.ai. "Workday is the operational heart of the enterprise, and by bringing C1.ai's identity governance directly into that core, we'll be able to manage access for every human and agent in real time – making work faster without making it less secure."

**Managing Access for Every Worker and Agent in One Place**

Imagine a new hire spending their first few days filing tickets just to get access to Slack, Jira, and the finance dashboard – and then keeping that access long after changing roles. Workday and C1.ai will bring these processes together so that new hires get the right access when they start, employees gain and lose access as their roles change, and departing workers are removed promptly. This translates to less time waiting on system permissions for employees, fewer manual approvals for managers, and fewer open doors for attackers.

**Complete Visibility and Control for Agents**

Some agents act on behalf of an employee and should only reach part of what that person can, while others work on their own with broader access that needs close oversight. An agent that was retired last quarter shouldn't still be able to get in, and most companies don't yet have a way to manage access at that level of detail.

The Workday Agent System of Record already gives companies one place to see and manage their agents. C1.ai will build on that, going beyond registering agents to also governing what they can access across Workday and other enterprise systems. Once registered, an agent can receive the access it needs, when it needs it, and lose that access immediately when its work or role ends.

**Transaction Details & Availability**

The transaction is expected to close in the fourth quarter of Workday's fiscal year 2027, ending January 31, 2027, subject to the satisfaction of customary closing conditions. BofA Securities is serving as financial advisor to Workday and Orrick is serving as its legal advisor. Qatalyst is serving as financial advisor to C1.ai and Ashurst Perkins Coie is serving as its legal advisor.

## Workday Launches Autopilot Agents That Get Real Work Done Across HR, Finance, and IT

**URL:** https://newsroom.workday.com/2026-10-13-Workday-Launches-Autopilot-Agents-That-Get-Real-Work-Done-Across-HR,-Finance,-and-IT

* [Download PDF](https://newsroom.workday.com/2026-10-13-Workday-Launches-Autopilot-Agents-That-Get-Real-Work-Done-Across-HR%2C-Finance%2C-and-IT?asPDF=1 "Download PDF")

*New Agents Continuously Handle Routine HR, Finance, and IT Processes in the Background, Allowing Employees to Offload Repetitive Workflows While Remaining Firmly in Control*

*Workday Agents Follow Each Company's Policies, Permissions, and Approvals So They Can be Trusted to Act, Not Just Answer Questions*

LAS VEGAS, Oct. 13, 2026 /[PRNewswire](http://www.prnewswire.com/)/ -- **Workday Rising 2026 –** [Workday, Inc.](https://www.workday.com/en-us/homepage.html) (NASDAQ: WDAY), the trusted agentic platform for HR, finance, and IT, today announced new AI agents that work as autopilots across hiring, payroll, accounting, and compliance. For HR, finance, and IT teams, this means less time chasing work and more time on the decisions only people can make.

[![](https://mmx.prnewswire.com/media/MS2012011/talent_acqusition_agent-2160p-1.mp4?id=OA3015068&p=thumbnail)](https://mmx.prnewswire.com/media/MS2012011/talent_acqusition_agent-2160p-1.mp4)

A recruiter managing 25 open jobs no longer needs to check each one to see which are running short on candidates. Workday's [Talent Acquisition Agent](https://www.workday.com/en-us/ai-agents/hr/talent-acquisition.html) is always checking the queue, finding qualified candidates as soon as the job is posted, and providing information about those candidates and their qualifications for the posted job to recruiters. Early adopter customers can use this autopilot capability starting today.

Workday is bringing the same autopilot capabilities to the Payroll, Accounting, and Deployment Agents customers already use. The [Payroll Agent](https://www.workday.com/en-us/ai-agents/hr/payroll.html) will spot missing pay data before payroll runs. The [Accounting Agent](https://www.workday.com/en-us/ai-agents/finance/accounting.html) will continuously test financial information, instead of waiting for a scramble when auditors ask for records, and the [Deployment Agent](https://www.workday.com/en-us/ai-agents/it-dev/it-admin.html) will flag new capabilities that can improve customer outcomes based on how a company has Workday set up.

**Lawful by Design**

Letting an agent work in the background only makes sense if a company can trust it. This matters most in the work that cannot afford to be wrong. In payroll, almost right means someone gets paid incorrectly. In the monthly close when the books are finalized, it means the numbers don't add up.

Workday agents are lawful by design. They inherit each company's policies, permissions, and approval rules from day one. Workday agents anticipate what needs attention, advise on the next step with the company's policy already applied, and act within each customer's controls.

Workday's agents will be orchestrated by [Sana](https://www.workday.com/en-us/ai-agents/sana.html), Workday's agentic experience and orchestration engine. Sana can pick up what needs to be done, coordinate the right Workday agents behind the scenes, and bring people in when review, approval, or judgment is needed. Employees don't need to know which agent handles what, or even ask where to start. Sana can keep the work moving and shows people where they need to act.

**Customers Decide What Agents Do on Their Own**

Customers choose which tasks run automatically and how far each agent can go. They decide which actions an agent can take on its own, which need review, and when a person makes the final call. Every action an agent takes is logged, so customers can see what it did, what information it used, and who approved it. They can also set how often agents run, so they can manage both activity and cost as agent use grows.

"Probabilistic AI is great at guessing, but in payroll and finance, a good guess isn't good enough," said Gerrit Kazmaier, president, product and technology, Workday. "Our agents can work in the background because they follow the same policies, permissions, and approvals that already run the business. That's what lets them go beyond making suggestions to anticipate, advise, and act autonomously."

"Until now, AI has worked like a copilot – helpful, but only when you ask it something. That's not enough anymore," said Joel Hellermark, chief AI officer, Workday. "Our agents work like autopilots – they get the work done on their own, and bring people in exactly when their judgment makes the difference."

**Talent Acquisition Agent Starts Working Before a Role is** **Opened**

The Talent Acquisition Agent keeps watch across open roles, flags those that are moving forward and those that may be at risk of missing their fill date, and starts assisting with requisitions before a recruiter logs into the system.

When applicants come in, the recruiter gets information about qualified candidates and their qualifications for the posted job as well as potential gaps. If the pipeline is too thin, the agent can notify recruiters in tools they already use, such as Slack, with timely sourcing actions and draft outreach for review. Sourcing runs in the background, but outreach to candidates still requires recruiter approval before anything is sent.

The Talent Acquisition Agent can also coordinate interview scheduling, give interviewers relevant context, and draft feedback for quick review. Recruiter preferences, like interview timing or format, can be saved to the agent's memory, personalizing the experience as the agent learns how each team works best.

The Talent Acquisition Agent works across the tools teams already use, including Workday Recruiting, HiredScore, Paradox, native Workday skills, and connected sourcing providers. The organization decides which actions can run on their own and which still require a person.

Time to fill moves from weeks to days. Recruiters have more capacity to focus on the conversations that move hiring forward.

**Payroll Agent Will Catch Problems Before Payroll Runs**

Workday agents are already helping payroll teams work faster. The Payroll Agent will go a step further. It will be able to keep checking payroll and catch problems before payroll runs, giving teams one clear view of where payroll stands.

For example, a series of organizational changes mean an audit of retroactive adjustments is critical. The agent can run the audit, flag anything outside of defined parameters, and draft any needed communications to the employees for the payroll team to review.

**Accounting Agent Will Help Keep the Books Audit-Ready With Continuous Financial Testing**

When a new customer signs a contract, the Accounting Agent can automatically extract key details, prepare accounting entries, recommend revenue recognition and billing schedules, and remind teams as payment deadlines approach. The agent is able to prepare the work, while accounting teams stay responsible for review, judgment, and decisions.

The agent can also help teams stay audit-ready throughout the year. It can test relevant transactions in the background, identifying issues and providing guidance to the audit team, helping to move an audit from a periodic scramble to a proactive control. That means accounting teams have more time for the work that needs judgment and less time chasing paperwork.

**Deployment Agent Will Help Customers Adopt New Capabilities from Every Release**

Since Workday announced the Deployment Agent last September, nearly 5,000 customers have activated it to simplify how they set up, update, and manage Workday.

Every Workday release brings new capabilities, but adopting them across a large environment takes time and planning. Deployment Agent will analyze how customers are currently using Workday and identify high-impact capabilities they haven't activated yet for their consideration.

For example, when a new release includes a capability that matches a customer's industry or workflow, Deployment Agent will flag it, explain what it does, project the operational impact, create a step-by-step configuration and testing plan, and prepare the setup so teams can review and approve adoption. This will transform how customers respond to every release, helping to  move rollouts from months of planning to weeks of execution.

Deployment Agent also continues to help with regulatory and compliance changes, flagging when new laws or policies may affect how a company has Workday set up and drafting a plan to address them.

"Agents are moving from singles pickleball – one human and one agent – to an orchestra phase, where the human is the director and agents run in the background, proactively doing the work," Holger Mueller, vice president and principal analyst, Constellation Research. "Workday's agents for talent acquisition, payroll, accounting, and more are built for that shift, working as autopilots while people stay in charge."

**Availability**

Autopilot capabilities in the Talent Acquisition Agent are available today to early adopter customers with general availability planned for early 2027. Autopilot capabilities for the Payroll, Accounting, and Deployment Agents are planned to be available to early adopter customers in early calendar year 2027.

*Sana Concierge Resolves Employee HR and IT Requests Before They Become Tickets*

LAS VEGAS, Oct. 13, 2026 /[PRNewswire](http://www.prnewswire.com/)/ -- **Workday Rising 2026** – [Workday, Inc.](http://www.workday.com/) (NASDAQ: WDAY), the trusted agentic platform for HR, finance, and IT, today announced that [Sana](https://www.workday.com/en-us/ai-agents/sana.html) is the new Workday Home, an agentic experience that brings tasks, decisions, meetings, and agent activity into one place, helping employees move directly from what needs attention to getting it done. Employees simply ask for what they need in natural language, and Sana orchestrates Workday agents to complete the work. Workday also introduced [Sana Concierge](https://www.workday.com/en-us/ai-agents/sana/concierge.html), a new HR and IT service agent that resolves employee requests – from onboarding and leave to password resets – before they become tickets.

[![](https://mmx.prnewswire.com/media/MS2011984/Sana-Unified-Experience-Demo.mp4?id=OA3015004&p=thumbnail)](https://mmx.prnewswire.com/media/MS2011984/Sana-Unified-Experience-Demo.mp4)

Most AI co-pilots at work can answer questions, but Sana can take action, from approving an expense, to processing a leave request, or resetting a password. What makes this possible is the combination of Workday's unique context – a company's people, pay, policies, permissions, and business processes – with the guardrails built into Workday's trusted agent platform. Every action follows a company's existing rules, with people brought in when judgment or approval is required.

"AI is the new UI," said Joel Hellermark, chief AI officer, Workday. "Users shouldn't have to decide which agent to use, just as they shouldn't have to click between dozens of different systems. With Sana at the heart of Workday, we're giving our customers an agentic experience that opens up a whole new way to work."

**A Simpler, More Personalized Way to Get Work Done**

Each employee's Home experience is personalized around their role, schedule, permissions, and work. When employees open the new Workday Home, they can immediately see the tasks and decisions that need their attention, understand why they matter, and take action from the same experience.

Sana also adapts the interface to the task instead of presenting the same static screen for every request. Powered by [Jev](https://typesafe.ai/blog/introducing-system-one-models-and-jev), a cutting-edge new intelligence model from TypeSafe AI, the experience comes alive in a way that is dynamic and prevents unnecessary clicks. With Jev, Sana can generate forms, calendars, approval cards, visualizations, and other interactive experiences in real time. An employee checking their next paycheck sees the amount and date immediately. If they need to schedule a meeting or request time off, Sana presents the right interface – a live calendar or date picker – and completes the task once the employee confirms.

The same Sana experience supports work that unfolds over days or weeks. A manager preparing a performance review, a finance team closing the quarter, or an HR leader building a workforce plan can see the relevant information, agent activity, outstanding tasks, and approvals in one persistent view. Sana keeps track of the work as it moves between people, agents, and systems, so teams can pick up where they left off instead of starting over.

**Sana Concierge: One Place for Every HR and IT Request**

Earlier this year, Workday announced [new self-service capabilities](https://newsroom.workday.com/2026-03-17-Introducing-Sana-from-Workday-Superintelligence-for-Work-That-Finds-Answers%2C-Takes-Action%2C-and-Automates-Workflows) and [Sana for IT Service Management](https://newsroom.workday.com/2026-05-21-Workday-Announces-Sana-for-IT-Service-Management-and-New-Travel-Agent). Sana Concierge will bring those capabilities together with Workday Help and Case Agent in one AI-native employee-service experience spanning HR and IT. It can resolve requests such as onboarding, offboarding, leave and payroll questions, password resets, and application access – without employees needing to know where to file a ticket or who to contact.

For example, an employee planning parental leave might otherwise need to find the right policy, check their time-off balance, contact payroll, and coordinate coverage with their manager. With Sana Concierge, they can simply ask: "I'm expecting a baby in November. How much time do I get, how will my pay work, and what do I need to do next?" Concierge can find the relevant policy, bring in the employee's Workday information, guide them through the required steps, and coordinate the appropriate approvals across HR, payroll, and their manager.

Most employee-service systems create a ticket and send it to a person. Sana Concierge is designed to resolve requests itself by applying the relevant policies and permissions and taking authorized action across Workday and connected systems. When human judgment is needed, the specialist receives the case history, supporting information, and recommended next steps, while the employee gets one place to complete the journey. Sana also gets smarter with every resolved request, proactively recommending new articles and automations that, once approved, help resolve future requests automatically.

**AI Built on Workday's Guardrails and Trusted Processes**

In HR and finance, almost right isn't good enough. Sana combines AI reasoning with Workday's deterministic business processes, which are designed to deliver consistent, auditable outcomes. Sana coordinates Workday agents that operate within each organization's policies, permissions, and approval processes, whether Workday, a customer, or a partner built them.

"Workday's bet with Sana is that HR and finance leaders won't settle for AI that answers questions – they need AI that finishes the work, within the guardrails their organization has already set," said Stacia Garr, co-founder, Red Thread Research. "That's the right bet, and Workday is positioned to execute on it in a way that is safe, secure, and effective for organizations."

*One Connection Carries Each Company's Permissions, Policies, and Approvals, So Workday's HR and Finance Agents Can Get the Work Done Wherever Work Happens*

**LAS VEGAS, October 13, 2026 – Workday Rising 2026 –** [Workday, Inc.](https://www.workday.com/en-us/homepage.html) (NASDAQ: WDAY), the trusted agentic platform for HR, finance, and IT, today announced that [Sana](https://www.workday.com/en-us/ai-agents/sana.html), Workday’s agentic experience and orchestration engine, will work inside the AI tools and platforms employees already use, including Claude, Google Cloud’s Gemini Enterprise, Microsoft Copilot, Microsoft Teams, and Slack, building on the self-service capabilities already available today.

Sana checks permissions, uses company data to compare the options, and saves the chosen plan to Workday with a full audit trail. If approval is needed, Sana gathers the context first and gives the manager a clear recommendation to act on.

**Sana Applies Company Rules Before Acting**

Sana is more than a connector. Third-party AI tools and platforms never get raw access to Workday data – they can now access this data through Sana, and Sana does the reasoning, applies each company's own permissions, approvals, and policies, and carries out the work so actions are lawful by design and recorded, regardless of the tool it started in. Workday introduced self-service capabilities in these tools earlier this year, giving employees a fast way to ask a question and get an answer. With Sana, these experiences now move beyond answers:

* An employee asks for a specific outcome, and Sana coordinates the right Workday agents and tools behind the scenes to complete the work, without requiring the employee to know which agent handles what.
* Sana can also spot certain issues before an employee asks for help. For example, after a business trip, Sana can flag an unfiled expense in Claude, apply the company policy, and prepare the expense report for review. The employee can confirm it from the conversation and get back to work.

**One Connection Brings Workday Agents Into the Tools Employees Already Use**

Customers do not need to connect every tool to every agent one by one. With Sana, a single connection brings agents – whether they are built by Workday, a customer, or a partner – into the tools and platforms employees already use.

When Workday adds a new capability, it shows up across connected tools without employees having to learn a new system. Policy checks and approvals stay anchored in Workday, so work can start in any connected AI tool while the company’s controls remain in place.

**Sana Keeps Complex Work Moving**

Some work requires more than a single question and answer. Open enrollment, performance reviews, closing the quarter, and workforce planning all involve many people, steps, and decisions that unfold over time.

For that work, Sana provides a home experience where employees and managers can see what is happening, pick up where they left off, and keep the work moving. Work can start in any tool, move into Sana when a richer view is needed, and return to the employee’s flow of work with context intact.

For example, if a manager asks an agent to move someone into a role the manager has no authority to fill, the agent will be able to stop, explain why, and point to the right next step instead of making a change that violates policy.

Applying for a job shouldn't feel harder than finding the job itself. But too often, job seekers click apply and hit the same roadblocks: another account, a new password, and the same work history they have already entered on a resume or past application.

When job seekers give up before submitting, employers lose candidates they worked hard to attract. On average, 60% of candidates abandon applications because of length and friction. And with the average job search lasting 11 weeks, time saved on each application can add up quickly.

With Workday Candidate Profile, a job seeker can use their saved information to help complete future applications with just an email and a one-time code, with no new password to remember. Roughly 80% of the standard fields will already be filled in with their contact information, work history, education, and skills before they've typed a word, which is expected to make applying about 40% faster than filling out the same application by hand. They can review it, change anything they want, add more detail, or answer a question specific to that employer, like how they heard about the role, and then submit.

"We've heard the feedback from job seekers, and they're right," said Gerrit Kazmaier, president, product and technology, Workday. "Filling out an application should be simple and seamless and nobody should have to retype their resume every time they apply for a job. Workday Candidate Profile gives every job seeker a fast path when applying for their next role."

**Job Seekers Apply Faster, Without Starting Over**

Job seekers will see the Workday Candidate Profile sign-in option when they apply through a company's [job site](https://www.workday.com/en-us/products/talent-management/talent-acquisition.html). They will also be able to sign in and apply from their phone, helping the hourly and frontline workers who often apply for jobs that way.

Workday Candidate Profile doesn't decide who gets hired. It doesn't rank, recommend, or reject anyone. It only fills in information job seekers have chosen to save, and they decide what each employer sees.

"For as long as I've worked in talent acquisition, we've asked job seekers to prove who they are from scratch every time they apply. It's one of the most persistent sources of friction in hiring, and they feel it," said Kyle Lagunas, founder and principal analyst, Kyle & Co. "Workday Candidate Profile flips that. Job seekers bring their story with them, and recruiters start the conversation with context instead of a blank form. That's the kind of practical change that actually moves the experience forward."

**Employers Get More Finished Applications, Without Changing How They Hire**

Every application a job seeker gives up on halfway through is one an employer never sees. And when applications are incomplete, recruiters can spend extra time chasing missing information. With Workday Candidate Profile, more people will finish the applications they start, submit more complete information, and leave a better first impression, whether or not they ultimately get the job.

Employers will not need to change how they hire. Their career site, screening questions, validations, and existing recruiting workflows all stay in place.

**Availability**

Workday Candidate Profile is expected to be generally available to Workday Recruiting customers in early calendar year 2027.
