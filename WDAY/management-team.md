# Workday, Inc. (WDAY) — Management & Governance

> Leadership reflects the **February 9, 2026 CEO transition**, when co-founder
> **Aneel Bhusri** returned as Chief Executive Officer and **Carl Eschenbach**
> stepped down.

## Aneel Bhusri — Co-Founder, Chief Executive Officer & Chair

Co-founded Workday in 2005 with Dave Duffield and has been its strategic and
product visionary ever since. Previously served as CEO (2009–2014 sole; 2014–2020
and 2020–2022 as co-CEO with Chano Fernandez), then as Executive Chair. On
**February 9, 2026 he returned as CEO**, framing AI as "a bigger transformation
than SaaS" and the reason for his return to lead Workday's "next chapter." Before
Workday he was a senior executive and product leader at **PeopleSoft** and a
partner at venture firm **Greylock Partners** (where he backed enterprise-software
companies). He remains Chair of the Board. Widely regarded as one of enterprise
software's most influential founders.

## Dave Duffield — Co-Founder, Chairman Emeritus

Serial enterprise-software entrepreneur who founded **PeopleSoft** (1987) and,
earlier, Integral Systems and Business Records Corporation. Co-founded Workday
after Oracle's hostile acquisition of PeopleSoft in 2005 and funded its early
years. No longer in day-to-day management; remains associated with the company as
a founder and major long-term shareholder.

## Carl Eschenbach — Former CEO, Strategic Advisor to the CEO

Served as CEO from 2023 to February 2026 (co-CEO with Bhusri in 2023, then sole
CEO from January 2024). Credited with instilling **operating discipline**,
expanding operating margins, broadening industry focus and international reach,
and laying AI groundwork. Previously President & COO of **VMware** and a General
Partner at **Sequoia Capital**; long-tenured Workday board member before becoming
CEO. Stepped down as CEO and from the board in Feb 2026 and now supports Bhusri as
strategic advisor.

## Zane Rowe — Chief Financial Officer

Joined Workday as CFO in **December 2023**. A veteran enterprise-software and
capital-markets CFO: previously CFO (and interim CEO) of **VMware**, CFO of
**EMC**, and CFO of **United Airlines**, with earlier finance leadership at
**Continental Airlines** and **Apple**. Oversees Workday's shift toward margin
expansion, disciplined capital allocation, and the enlarged share-repurchase
program.

## Gerrit Kazmaier — President, Product & Technology

Leads Workday's product, engineering and platform organization, including the AI
(Illuminate/agentic) roadmap. Joined from **Google Cloud**, where he was VP/GM of
Data Analytics, Databases and Looker; earlier a senior product executive at
**SAP** (HANA, analytics). Succeeded the prior co-president product-and-technology
structure.

## Rob Enslin — President & Chief Commercial Officer

Assumed the President & CCO role on **December 2, 2024**, responsible for global
sales, partnerships and customer experience. A veteran go-to-market leader:
formerly co-CEO of **UiPath**, President of **Google Cloud**, and a decades-long
SAP executive (President of SAP Cloud Business Group / SAP North America). Drives
Workday's revenue growth and partner ecosystem.

## Mark Hawkins — Vice Chair & Lead Independent Director

Named Vice Chair and Lead Independent Director in the 2026 transition. Former CFO
of **Salesforce**, **Autodesk** and **Logitech**; brings enterprise-software
finance and governance depth to board oversight during the leadership handoff.

## Corporate Governance Notes

- **Dual-class share structure:** Class A (one vote) and Class B (super-voting)
  common stock. The founders (Bhusri and Duffield) and insiders control a
  disproportionate share of voting power via Class B, giving founders durable
  control despite a minority economic stake. As of Jan 31, 2026 there were ~212M
  Class A and ~47M Class B shares outstanding (Class B converts to Class A on
  transfer and is declining over time).
- **Board leadership:** Chair (Bhusri) is also CEO after Feb 2026; independent
  oversight is anchored by a **Lead Independent Director / Vice Chair** (Hawkins).
- **Capital return:** no dividend; capital returned through **share
  repurchases** (~$2.9B in FY2026), partially offsetting heavy stock-based-comp
  dilution (~$1.6B/yr).
- **Convertible notes:** ~$3.0B of convertible senior notes outstanding fund the
  balance sheet alongside ~$4–8B of cash and marketable securities.
