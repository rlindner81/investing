**Fundamentals have since eased those fears.** The May 21, 2026 Q1 print beat (EPS \$2.66 vs \$2.51 est; revenue \$2.54B, +13%), the company raised its FY operating-margin outlook to 30.5%, and shares jumped ~10% on results that "quieted fears of AI disruption" (Bloomberg). Bhusri is repositioning WDAY as an AI disruptor, not a victim — agentic AI ARR crossed ~\$400–500M, he cut internal agent count from ~50 to ~20, stood up an AI task force, and is pushing a "think like a startup again" product-first reset.

**Upcoming catalyst:** next earnings ~Aug 19, 2026 (fiscal Q2, ending Jul 31). Company guided Q2 subscription revenue to \$2.455B (+13%) and 30.0% non-GAAP operating margin; Street models EPS ~\$2.62 (+18.6% Y/Y) on revenue ~\$2.63B.

**Analyst posture:** broadly constructive but cautious — 17 Buy / 16 Hold / 0 Sell, average PT ~\$171 (well above spot). Macro is a headwind: a hawkish new Fed chair (Warsh) is pressuring high-multiple software multiples broadly. The core bear case remains structural — whether agentic AI erodes seat-based HCM/financials demand. The bull case: durable ~14% growth, expanding margins, and a valuation near five-year lows.

**Rating:** +0.7

## Verdict

Workday is a high-quality, cash-generative compounder (~14% revenue growth, 32% company FCF margin) whose multiple has de-rated to five-year lows — TTM P/S 3.9x and P/FCF ~12x — on AI-disruption fears rather than fundamental deterioration. The May quarter beat and raised margin guidance, an impeccable guidance track record, and Bhusri's AI-first reset are stabilizing the story, and price action has snapped back +9–10% over recent weeks. The main risks are structural (does agentic AI erode seat-based demand?), the heavy SBC load (~half of reported FCF, though buybacks are shrinking the share count), persistent insider selling, and a still-declining 200-day SMA signaling the primary trend hasn't turned. A clean Q2 beat-and-raise on Aug 19 with continued agentic-AI ARR momentum would confirm the recovery and support a higher rating; renewed guidance softness or evidence of AI-driven churn would send it back toward the lows.

**Rating:** +0.7
