# WDAY — Q3 FY2027 Print (Plan)

**The event:** Q3 FY2027 (quarter ends 2026-10-31). Date not yet announced; expect
**Tue 2026-11-24 after close**, the Tuesday of Thanksgiving week, as in the last
three years. That puts the reaction bar on **Wed Nov 25**. Thu Nov 26 is closed and
Fri Nov 27 is a thin half-day, so "within two sessions" below means **Nov 25 and
Mon Nov 30**.

**Why it matters:** the analyst day ended in scenario B/D: FY2028 held at \~11%,
Flex Credits still counted in customers rather than dollars, no reaction. This
print is the follow-up that plan deferred to. If it is qualitative again, the
waiting is over. See [AnalystDay2026.md](AnalystDay2026.md).

## The bar

- **Subscription revenue:** guide \$2,515M (+12%). Workday routinely beats by a
  little, so \~\$2,525–2,530M is "in line", not a beat.
- **Non-GAAP operating margin:** guide 30.0%. GAAP margin carries the \$55–70M
  restructuring charge; ignore it.
- **cRPO:** +14.2% in Q2. Under 12% would be the first real crack.
- **Total subscription backlog:** +8.0% vs. revenue +13.9% in Q2. Narrowing or
  widening that gap is the leading signal for FY2028.
- **Flex Credits customers:** \~300 at Q2, with \~200 added in that quarter. Ignore
  the 5,500 "organic agent" figure, which is mostly the Deployment Agent.
- **FY2028 language:** "\~11% with some upside" is the baseline. Listen for whether
  it firms up, repeats, or gets hedged.

Reference at \$188.77: \$45.5B market cap, 4.1x FY2028 subscription @11%, 12.6x
FY2028 FCF/share (\$15).

## Execution — tranches 2 and 3

**Before the print: no orders.** A resting limit fills the same way in A and B,
and that erases the distinction this plan depends on. Tranche 1 is already out
(filled at \$210 on 2026-09-03), so this plan covers the remaining two thirds.

**A — Firm-up.** Hold both tranches. Set a tranche 2 GTC limit scaled to how strong the print is:

- **Clear A** (a real Flex Credits dollar figure plus cRPO ≥14%): **\$225**, \~15.0x
  FY2028 FCF/share. **Place it only after the reaction day closes**, effective
  Fri Nov 27 (the first session after the Nov 25 reaction bar). It shouldn't fill
  on the reaction-day spike; the point is to sell into follow-through.
- **Marginal A** (clears the thresholds and nothing more, or a bigger customer
  count with no dollars): **\$210**, \~14.0x and just under the post-Q2 high of
  \$211.83. The top of A's +6–12% reaction range can fill it on the day.

These are deliberately below the \$227.49 high from the Silver Lake leak, which was
a deal-rumor price. If the order doesn't fill, hold to the Q4 print. In a confirmed
scenario, that is fine. Tranche 3 holds to the Q4
print (late Feb 2027), where formal FY2028 guidance lands.

**B — In-line, still qualitative.** This is the second event in a row without
monetization numbers, so the hold thesis is no longer earning its wait.
**Sell tranche 2** into print-day strength. Failing that, sell at market by
**Fri Dec 4**. Don't chase a better price. **Tranche 3** holds to the Q4 print as
the final test of the FY2028 guide.

**C — Crack.** **Sell tranches 2 and 3 at market** within two sessions (Nov 25 /
Nov 30). Don't set a limit above the market, and don't wait for a bounce.

**D — Deal surprise.** Cancel everything and reassess against the deal case in
[SilverLakeTakeover.md](SilverLakeTakeover.md). Do not run this plan on top of a
live bid.

**Mixed signals:** if revenue and the metrics point different ways, the metrics win.
A revenue beat with cRPO under 12% is C. A soft revenue print with Flex Credits
dollars disclosed is A.
