# WDAY — Financial Analyst Day, 2026-10-13 (Plan and Outcome)

**The event:** Workday's Financial Analyst Day, held inside Rising (Las Vegas,
Oct 12–15). The prior one was September 2025 — that is where the \$5B repurchase
plan came from, which Rowe said on the Q2 FY2027 call was completed six months
early. Bhusri's first analyst day since returning as CEO in February 2026; his
\$138.8M package has \$75M tied to five-year stock-price targets, so he has direct
incentive to make this the venue where he answers the AI-disruption thesis.

**Why it is pre-planned:** on the Q2 FY2027 call (2026-08-27) Rowe pointed at the
event three times and gave a deliberately partial FY2028 preview — **\~11%
subscription growth, at least +200bps margin** — framed as "early context ahead
of our upcoming Financial Analyst Day." The formal multi-year framework lands
Oct 13. Deciding the responses in advance is the point: the same price action
means opposite things depending on *what* is disclosed, and that distinction is
impossible to draw while watching a green candle.

## Outcome (written 2026-10-14)

**Verdict: scenario B content, scenario D price action → act on D.** What was
disclosed matches B: FY2028 held at \~11%, margin formalized, Flex Credits still
customer counts. But B's "+3–7% pop on cost discipline" never happened, so there
is nothing to sell tranche 2 *into*. B's sell case was about not paying 5.8x TTM /
17.1x FCF for margin on decelerating growth. At \$189 the market isn't asking that
price (4.8x / 14.3x). **Hold tranches 2 and 3, place no new orders; the late-November
Q3 print carries the information.** This is the "If Oct 13 is qualitative" branch
in "Sequencing" below.

**The real tell was Bhusri on timing.** On revenue from agents he said "You'll see
that maybe not this year, or I don't know when"; on the consumption flywheel,
"When exactly it happens, I can't give you a date". That is a candid
admission that the AI line stays unmodelable through FY2028. Tal Liani asked
directly why subscription growth keeps decelerating despite AI. Rowe cited renewal
cycles, M&A laps and international, and said customers "have to adopt" before the
model shifts. No stabilization call.

### New information the plan didn't anticipate

- **C1.ai acquisition** (ConductorOne, agentic identity governance). Terms
  undisclosed, closes Q4 FY2027 (by Jan 31, 2027). The pitch: HR is the upstream
  source of identity, so Workday governs access for both people and agents. This
  pushes Workday into security and toward the CIO as a third buyer. Bhusri: "It is
  going to take us a little bit of time to ramp up the sales." He calls identity and
  ITSM "upsides in the model", meaning they are **not** in the 11%.
- **AI ARR \~\$600M**, more than 3x y/y. 25% of core customers have bought at least
  one AI SKU, up from \~10% a year ago. HiredScore, Paradox and Extend AI are each
  over \$100M, and Sana is approaching it. **\~900 customers bought at least one agent
  in the TTM, with \~12% uplift** on that cohort. Two case studies showed +40% and
  +50% ARR uplift.
- **Installed-base mix.** AI was just over 50% of incremental customer-base ARR in
  Q2. The expectation is \~70% of the base selling motion is AI-driven in FY2028.
  The installed base is 60% of TTM subscription revenue.
- **Sana Enterprise Lighthouse:** 18 large brands get **one year of licensing
  waived** in exchange for co-innovation and references. A small near-term revenue
  giveaway that runs straight through FY2028.
- **TAM** raised to \$276B (from \$188B), including \$100B for AI/platform (more than
  2x). ITSM and identity come on top of that.
- **Headless/API economics.** Bhusri expects API usage to be "margin neutral" (lower
  selling cost). Anthropic integrates via Sana rather than raw APIs, and MCP access
  requires an Extend AI license. Extend → Extend AI conversions carry a \~20%
  uplift. Workday Data Cloud has closed 20 deals.
- **Medium enterprise.** Net-new deal sizes up \~20% y/y. Workday GO volume rose 5x
  q/q in Q2. Bhusri expects UKG share gains as the deployment agent cuts
  implementation cost.
- **Pre-event 8-Ks.** On 2026-09-29: a **2.5% workforce reduction**, mostly in
  Product & Technology, with \$65–80M charges (\$40–55M cash), all excluded from
  non-GAAP; the FY2027 guide was reiterated. Bhusri framed it as removing layers,
  "not for cost cutting". On 2026-10-01: a new **\$1.5B revolver** to 2031,
  replacing \$1B, undrawn.
- **Customer token-cost worry.** Bhusri said four customers told him that day they
  worry about the token cost of agents. He spun it as demand, but it is also a
  ceiling on consumption pricing.

## The one question that decides everything

**Does Rowe put a number on Flex Credits monetization, and does FY2028
subscription growth come in above 11%?**

Everything below branches off that. The hold thesis is AI monetization, and today
only **\~200 of 5,500** organic-agent customers are on Flex Credits — the
consumption meter. Oct 13 either converts that from a story into a modeled line
or it does not. Gabriela Borges asked on the Q2 call how many credits an
onboarding or procurement agent actually consumes and what that means for net
expansion; she did not get a number. That answer is the event.

**Multiple-recovery is not the thesis.** The old 8–10x P/S was priced on 17–19%
growth; FY2028 is pre-guided at \~11%. The 2024 range is a ceiling that no longer
applies, not a target. The bull case is earnings power — 31%→33% margins, FCF
+15%, share count 266.9M → 241.2M in eight quarters (−9.6%) — not re-rating.

## Scenarios

### A — Growth confirmed (\~25%)

FY2028 subscription guided **≥13%**; explicit Flex Credits unit economics (credits
per agent, attach rate, expansion per customer); AI contribution to FY2028 ARR
quantified. Likely +6–12% on the day.

**Hold tranches 2 and 3. Cancel any standing sell.** This is the thesis being
confirmed, not an exit. At 13% growth + 33% margins + \~4%/yr share shrink that is
\~17% per-share compounding, and \$230 is only 4.9x forward FY2028 sales.
**Revisit at \$250** (5.4x FY2028, 19x FY2027 FCF) or the late-November Q3 print,
whichever comes first. If it gaps straight through \$250 on the day, sell tranche
2 there — that is the multiple pricing in success not yet delivered.

### B — Margin pop, growth still qualitative (\~40%, most likely)

FY2028 held at \~11%; +200bps margin formalized or raised; possibly a larger
buyback authorization; Flex Credits still described in customer counts and
enthusiasm rather than dollars. Pop of +3–7% on cost discipline.

**Sell tranche 2 into it — limit \$225.** This is the pop pre-committed to
selling: a re-rating on cost discipline while the growth question goes unanswered
for another quarter. \$225 is 5.8x TTM sales and 17.1x the FY2027 FCF guide —
paying up for margin expansion on decelerating growth. If it pops but stalls
under \$225, sell tranche 2 at market within the following week rather than
chasing the exact number; the signal is the disclosure gap, not the price.
**Tranche 3 holds** to the November Q3 print, the first quarter with meaningful
Flex Credits conversion data.

### C — Disappointment (\~25%)

FY2028 at or below 11% with hedging; cRPO framed cautiously; Flex Credits
conversion slower than the 5,500 → 200 gap implies; or any hint the AI attach is
**substitutive rather than incremental** (Kirk Materne's question). Down 5–10%.

**Sell tranche 2 at market within two sessions. Do not set a limit above the
market.** If the growth call is failing, the reason to hold is gone and what
remains is 11%-and-falling at 15.5x FCF. Do not wait for a bounce to a nicer
print. **Tranche 3:** sell into any relief rally toward \$200; absent one within
\~3 weeks, exit at market before the Q3 print.

### D — No pop, no damage (\~10%)

In-line, tightly scripted, ±2%.

**Hold both tranches, place no new orders.** Non-event; the November print
carries the information.

- **Named sovereign anchor before Oct 13** — rated a white-swan event and the risk
  is accepted on tranche 1. If it happens anyway: cancel tranches 2 and 3 and
  reassess against a \$225–235 deal case. Do not run an analyst-day plan on top of
  a live bid. See [SilverLakeTakeover.md](SilverLakeTakeover.md).
- **Tranche 1 fills above \$210 on a sector melt-up** — fine, it did its job. Do not
  raise the limit chasing IGV; the point was locking the non-fundamental part of
  the move.

## What to watch on the call

- **Flex Credits in dollars vs. customer counts** — the single tell separating A from B.
- **FY2028 number vs. the 11% pre-guide** — Rowe called it "early context," which
  leaves room in both directions.
- **The Borges question** — does AI upside get offset by harder core-renewal
  pricing? An honest answer here is worth more than the headline number.
- **Total subscription backlog** — +8.0% in Q2 vs subscription revenue +13.9%. That
  gap closing or widening leads the revenue line by about a year.

## Sequencing

Oct 13 sets the framework; **late-November Q3** is the first quarter that tests it
with real conversion data. If Oct 13 is qualitative (scenario B or D), "wait for
new data" means waiting until November, not until October.
