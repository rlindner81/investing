# Lyft, Inc. (NASDAQ: LYFT) — Management Team

## Executive Leadership

---

### David Risher — Chief Executive Officer

**Background:**
- Became Lyft CEO in April 2023, replacing co-founders Logan Green (CEO) and John
  Zimmer (President), who stepped back from day-to-day roles into board positions
- Longtime technology executive with a customer-obsession pedigree: an early
  Microsoft executive, then one of Amazon's first senior hires — as Senior Vice
  President of US Retail he built and ran Amazon's core retail business in its
  formative years under Jeff Bezos
- Co-founded and led **Worldreader**, a nonprofit expanding digital reading access
  in the developing world, for over a decade before joining Lyft
- Joined Lyft's board before being named CEO

**Role and approach at Lyft:**
- Refocused Lyft on operational execution and a "customer-obsessed" culture,
  borrowing directly from the Amazon playbook — rider experience, pricing/ETA
  reliability, and driver earnings transparency
- Championed the shift to **free cash flow** as the company's headline financial
  goal, driving Lyft from cash burn to $1.1B FCF in FY2025
- Introduced driver-friendly commitments (e.g. the 70% weekly driver-earnings
  guarantee) and leaned into partnerships (Chase, DoorDash, United, Disney)
- Oversaw the 2025 acquisition of **FreeNow**, Lyft's first international
  expansion, and the company's autonomous-vehicle partnership strategy

---

### Erin Brewer — Chief Financial Officer

**Background:**
- Joined Lyft as CFO in 2024, succeeding Erin Brewer's predecessor in the role
  during Lyft's transition to sustained free-cash-flow generation
- Career finance executive with public-company CFO and senior finance experience
  prior to Lyft
- Primary financial spokesperson on earnings calls and in the quarterly investor
  letters/press releases

**Role at Lyft:**
- Owns financial strategy, capital allocation, and investor relations during the
  free-cash-flow inflection — including the $500M+ share repurchase program and
  the convertible-note refinancing (settling the 2025 notes, issuing 2029/2030
  converts with capped calls)
- Emphasizes Gross Bookings, Adjusted EBITDA margin (as a percentage of Gross
  Bookings), and trailing-twelve-month free cash flow as the framework by which
  Lyft guides and reports — Lyft guides Gross Bookings and Adjusted EBITDA, not a
  GAAP revenue dollar range

---

### Logan Green — Co-Founder & Board Member (former CEO)

**Background:**
- Co-founded Zimride in 2007 and launched the Lyft product in 2012; served as CEO
  through the 2019 IPO and until April 2023
- Conceived the original carpooling idea after experiencing informal shared-ride
  systems while traveling in Zimbabwe; long-focused on reducing car ownership and
  urban congestion
- Stepped back from the CEO role in 2023 as part of the leadership transition to
  David Risher; remains involved at the board level

---

### John Zimmer — Co-Founder & Board Member (former President)

**Background:**
- Co-founded Zimride/Lyft with Logan Green; served as President through the IPO
  and until the 2023 transition
- Background in hospitality (Cornell School of Hotel Administration) that shaped
  Lyft's early rider-experience and brand positioning (the signature pink
  branding, "your friend with a car")
- Stepped back alongside Green in 2023; remains on the board

## Corporate Governance Notes

- **Founder-led transition:** In April 2023, co-founders Logan Green and John
  Zimmer handed operational control to David Risher (an outside operator) while
  retaining board seats — an unusual, clean hand-off from founders to a
  professional CEO, motivated by a need to restore competitive execution against
  Uber.
- **Dual-class structure history:** Lyft went public with a dual-class share
  structure (Class A public shares, one vote; Class B founder shares, super-voting)
  that concentrated control with Green and Zimmer. The **~8.5M Class B founder
  shares fully converted to Class A during 2025** (no Class B outstanding from Q3
  2025 onward), simplifying the capital structure and reducing founder voting
  control.
- **Capital return:** Lyft initiated a share-repurchase program (authorization
  $500M+) once free cash flow turned durably positive — a notable shift for a
  company that had historically burned cash and diluted shareholders via SBC.
- **Headquarters:** San Francisco, California.
- **Board / strategic partners:** Toyota (via the 2021 sale of Lyft's Level 5
  self-driving unit to Woven Planet) and various commercial partners feature in
  Lyft's ecosystem, though Lyft is not controlled by any strategic holder.
