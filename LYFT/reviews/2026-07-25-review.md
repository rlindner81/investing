Y/Y revenue growth ran +27% to +40% through 2024 but stepped down to the +10–14% range across 2025, and the FY-2025 aggregate came in at +9.2% versus +31.4% in FY-2024. The most recent print, q1-2026, showed +13.8% Y/Y — a modest re-acceleration off the softest 2025 quarters, but the multi-year trend is clearly a maturing, mid-teens-or-lower grower rather than a hyper-growth story.

The FCF profile is the standout. Lyft is solidly FCF-positive and the trend is strong: company FCF grew from \$766M in FY-2024 to \$1,116M in FY-2025 (+45.6%), with TTM company FCF at \$1,122M — a 17.2% FCF margin. Even the conservative strict-ex-SBC measure is healthy at \$806M TTM (12.4% margin), up from \$435M in FY-2024. SBC is meaningful at ~\$316M TTM but is comfortably covered — the gap between company FCF (\$1,122M) and strict-ex-SBC FCF (\$806M) is entirely the SBC add-back plus minor extra capex, and both remain strongly positive.

Valuation is cheap on cash flow. TTM P/S is just 0.8x and P/FCF company is 4.8x (6.7x strict ex-SBC) at the \$14.20 reference price — well below where the stock traded through most of 2025 (P/FCF of 6–10x). Market cap is \$5.43B against \$1.03B cash and \$987M debt, so roughly net-cash-neutral. Guidance rows are unpopulated — Lyft guides on gross bookings, not revenue dollars — so forward P/S is n/m here.

A ~5x P/FCF with double-digit revenue growth, rising FCF, and a net-neutral balance sheet is inexpensive; the debate is whether growth durability and AV disruption justify the discount.

## 3. News

The dominant fresh catalyst is a **short report**. On July 23, 2026, LYFT fell ~5% after Bleecker Street published a short thesis flagging \$1.3B–\$2.7B of allegedly undisclosed legal liabilities. This is the proximate driver of the -8% down-week seen in the price table and the elevated, rising relative volume — the selling has a specific news catalyst rather than being generic sector weakness. The claim is unverified and contested, but it introduces a tail-risk overhang that will linger until management addresses it, likely on the August call.

**Analyst posture is mixed and drifting more cautious.** The blended price target rose over the past year from ~\$24 to ~\$32 on improving rides growth and a firmer gross-bookings outlook, but more recent revisions have pulled the average down toward ~\$27.71 on softer margin and growth assumptions. Recent individual moves: RBC Capital initiated/reiterated a Buy on July 22; Jefferies nudged its target to \$15.50 from \$15 on July 14 — a notably low target that sits near spot, underscoring the divided view. Consensus average is roughly in line with recent spot, i.e. limited near-term upside priced by the median analyst even as bulls see much higher.

**Upcoming catalysts.** Q2-2026 earnings are confirmed for **August 6, 2026, after close** (one source cites Aug 5) — the key event, consensus EPS ~\$0.17.
