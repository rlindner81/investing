# NFLX — Q3 2026 Print (Plan)

**The event:** Q3 2026 results **Tue 2026-10-20 after close**, so the reaction bar
is **Wed Oct 21**. "Within two sessions" below means Oct 21–22. Stake: **6% of
book**. Reference at \$70.57: \$294B market cap, 26.3x TTM FCF, 5.7x FY2026
revenue guide.

**Why it matters:** the 2026-09-09 review rated it HOLD (+0.2) and named this
print the swing factor. Since then: Sarandos said "not growing as fast as I want",
Wells Fargo (\$57) and Huber (\$70) moved to Underweight, Deutsche Bank (\$95) moved
to Buy, and 1,000 layoffs came with a memo saying "on track to deliver our full
year forecast". Q3 had already closed when that line was written, so a revenue
miss is unlikely. The debate is engagement, not this quarter's revenue.

## The bar

- **Revenue:** guide \$12.86B (+11.7%), consensus \$12.88B. NFLX has landed
  within ±1.2% of guide for eight quarters, so \$12.80–12.95B is "in line".
- **Operating margin:** guide 33.2%. The layoff severance most likely lands in
  Q4. Judge the margin excluding it.
- **Implied Q4:** FY \$51.0–51.4B minus Q1–Q3 = **\$13.33–13.73B**. A Q4 guide
  below \$13.33B is a disguised cut to the full year.
- **FY2026:** \$51.0–51.4B revenue, 31.5% margin. Held, raised, or cut.
- **Ads:** "on track for \~\$3B" repeated, or softened.
- **Engagement language:** there is no hard hours data until the January report.
  Listen for per-member viewing, US price-increase plans, and the H2 slate.
  Wells Fargo expects top-100 originals hours down \~21% in H2.
- **Capital allocation:** buyback pace, and any reopening of M&A.

## Scenarios

| | Scenario | Prob. | What it looks like | Reaction |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | Beat & firm | **20%** | Revenue ≥\$12.95B, margin ≥33.2%, Q4 guide ≥\$13.6B or FY raised, ads ahead of \~\$3B, engagement described as stabilizing | +6–12% |
| **B** | In line | **45%** | Revenue \$12.80–12.95B, FY held, Q4 guide in the implied range, engagement unaddressed or vague | ±5% |
| **C** | Crack | **25%** | Revenue <\$12.80B, FY cut, Q4 guide <\$13.33B, margin miss excluding severance, **or** management confirms falling per-member hours or pauses US pricing | −8–15% |
| **D** | Strategic surprise | **10%** | Large acquisition, change in capital-allocation policy, or a reorganization beyond the layoffs | — |

The last three prints reacted −2% / −10% / −7% on roughly in-line numbers. Read
B as skewed to the downside.

## Execution

**Before the print: no orders.** Hold 6%. A resting limit can't tell A from B.

**A — Beat & firm.** Hold 6%. Add **1 pt (to 7%, the cap)** only after the
reaction day closes, and only if the stock still closes above the Oct 21 open on
Fri Oct 23. Buy into follow-through, not the gap. Then hold to the Q4 print
(late Jan 2027).

**B — In line.** **Trim to 4%** (sell a third) at market by **Fri Oct 23**. Don't
wait for a bounce. The thesis "great business, fair price, dead money until it
grows" doesn't justify a 6% weight going into January's annual engagement
report, which Wells Fargo flags as the next negative catalyst. Hold the 4% to
that print.

**C — Crack.** **Cut to 2%** (sell two thirds) at market within two sessions
(Oct 21–22). Keep 2% as a valuation residual: at \~\$60 it is \~22x FCF. Reassess
it at the Q4 print. Don't set a limit above the market.

**D — Strategic surprise.** Freeze. Cancel nothing, place nothing, and reassess
the deal on its own merits before applying A–C. See
[CORPORATE_STRATEGY.md](CORPORATE_STRATEGY.md) for the M&A discipline baseline.

**Mixed signals:** the guide and engagement language outrank the Q3 revenue
figure. A revenue beat with a Q4 guide under \$13.33B is C. A revenue beat with
confirmation that per-member hours are falling is B at best. An in-line quarter
with a raised FY and firm ad commentary is A.
