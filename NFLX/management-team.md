# Netflix, Inc. (NASDAQ: NFLX) — Management Team

## Executive Leadership

Netflix runs under a co-CEO structure adopted in January 2023, when co-founder Reed Hastings stepped back from the CEO role to Executive Chairman.

---

### Ted Sarandos — Co-CEO

**Background:**
- Joined Netflix in 2000; became Chief Content Officer, then was named co-CEO in January 2023
- Grew up in Phoenix, Arizona; began his career managing a video-rental store, where he developed the deep catalog instincts that shaped his career
- Before Netflix, held roles at video distribution company ETD and at Video City / West Coast Video

**Role at Netflix:**
- Architect of Netflix's transformation into an original-content studio — greenlit *House of Cards*, *Orange Is the New Black*, *Stranger Things*, *Squid Game*, and the company's move into film, live events, and sports
- Leads all content, marketing, communications, publicity, and legal
- The primary voice on content strategy on earnings interviews
- Longtime relationship with Reed Hastings dating to 1999; widely seen as the creative-and-Hollywood half of the leadership pair

---

### Greg Peters — Co-CEO

**Background:**
- Joined Netflix in 2008; served as Chief Product Officer and then Chief Operating Officer before being named co-CEO in January 2023
- Earlier career in technology and international business development, including a role at Yahoo and time living/working in Japan (he speaks Japanese)

**Role at Netflix:**
- Leads product, technology, advertising, gaming, partnerships, and the day-to-day operating and financial engine
- Drove the launch and scaling of the ad-supported tier, the paid-sharing initiative, and the in-house ad-tech platform
- The primary voice on product, monetization, and operational strategy on earnings interviews
- Seen as the product-and-operations half of the leadership pair

---

### Spencer "Spence" Neumann — Chief Financial Officer

**Background:**
- Joined Netflix as CFO in January 2019
- Previously CFO of Activision Blizzard; before that spent nearly two decades at The Walt Disney Company in senior finance roles, including CFO of Disney Parks and Resorts

**Role at Netflix:**
- Oversees all financial strategy, capital allocation, and investor relations
- Has guided Netflix through its transition from a cash-burning content investor into a large free-cash-flow generator, funding share repurchases while maintaining disciplined leverage
- Presents financial results and guidance on the quarterly earnings interviews alongside the co-CEOs

---

### Reed Hastings — Co-Founder & Chairman (departing)

**Background:**
- Co-founded Netflix in 1997 with Marc Randolph; served as CEO from 1998 until January 2023, then as Executive Chairman
- Earlier founded and sold Pure Software; holds a master's in computer science from Stanford; served in the Marine Corps and the Peace Corps

**Role and departure:**
- Built Netflix's distinctive culture of "freedom and responsibility," high performance, and candor (documented in his book *No Rules Rules*)
- Pioneered the shift from DVD-by-mail to streaming and then to global original programming
- In April 2026, informed the board he will **not stand for re-election** at the June 2026 Annual Meeting, in order to focus on philanthropy and other pursuits — ending his direct governance role while remaining the company's founder and biggest champion

### Lowell Singer — VP, Investor Relations

## Corporate Governance Notes

- **Co-CEO structure:** Adopted January 2023; Ted Sarandos (content) and Greg Peters (product/operations) share the chief-executive role, a deliberate succession design that let founder Reed Hastings step back gradually.
- **Single-class shares:** Unlike many founder-led tech peers, Netflix has a single class of common stock with one vote per share — no dual-class super-voting structure. This gives public shareholders proportionate voting power.
- **Board transition:** Reed Hastings' decision not to seek re-election at the June 2026 Annual Meeting marks the end of founder involvement at the board level.
- **10-for-1 stock split:** Effected November 14, 2025, lowering the nominal per-share price (into the ~\$100 range post-split) to broaden accessibility; economically neutral.
- **Earnings communication:** Netflix does not hold a traditional analyst conference call. It publishes a detailed shareholder letter and hosts a pre-recorded-style live video interview on YouTube, where co-CEOs, the CFO, and IR answer questions submitted in advance by sellside analysts.
- **Capital allocation:** Priorities are (1) reinvestment in the business organically and via selective M&A, (2) maintaining liquidity, and (3) returning excess cash to shareholders through buybacks. The share-repurchase program was paused during the 2026 Warner Bros. pursuit and resumed after Netflix declined to raise its offer.
