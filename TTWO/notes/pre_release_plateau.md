# Pre-Release Plateau — TTWO into major Rockstar launches

**Thesis.** Ahead of its two biggest generational launches (GTA V, 2013-09-17;
Red Dead Redemption 2, 2018-10-26) TTWO ran up, topped out **~3–4 weeks before
release** in a tight **~3-day plateau near +23% above its T-100 base**, then
**faded into launch day**. The pre-release plateau — not the release itself — is
the actionable exit-watch window. This note pins the analog to exact **GTA VI
(2026-11-19)** calendar dates.

**Method.** Trading-day-relative-to-release axis (`T=0` = first trading bar
on/after release). Each series indexed to 100 at **T-100 trading days**. Prices:
TTWO daily closes from `prices/daily/TTWO.csv` (vendor split-adjusted). GTA VI
calendar built forward/backward from 2026-11-19 over NYSE trading days
(weekends + Labor Day 2026-09-07 removed; no NYSE closures fall in the peak
window).

## 1. Pre-release peaks (max close, indexed to T-100 = 100)

| Game | Peak date | Trading days to release | Index | Close |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| **GTA V** | 2013-08-26 | **T-15** | 123.1 | \$19.10 |
| **RDR2**  | 2018-09-28 | **T-20** | 122.6 | \$137.99 |

Both topped ~**+23%** over the T-100 base, ~5 years apart — strikingly
consistent. The peak sits in the **T-20 → T-15** window (~3–4 weeks pre-launch).

## 2. Plateau length (contiguous run at an elevated level)

Threshold-independent framing (fixed index level, matching the "120+" zone):

| Game | 120+ plateau | Length | 118+ (looser) |
|---|---|---:|---|
| **GTA V** | T-17 → T-15 (Aug 22–26 '13) | **3 td** | same (3 td) |
| **RDR2**  | T-21 → T-19 (Sep 27–Oct 1 '18) | **3 td** | T-24 → T-17 (8 td) |

At a strict **120+** cutoff both plateaus are **~3 trading days**. RDR2 widens to
8 td only at 118+. Honest read: a **~3-day plateau at 120+** nested inside a
broader ~1–2 week band in the 118–123 zone. After the plateau, **both faded into
launch** (final week: GTA V −6.3%, RDR2 −2.0% into T=0).

## 4. Half-plateau confirmation date (GTA VI)

Rule: plateau entry + half the plateau length. If GTA VI is *still holding* the
elevated zone that far in, the pattern is confirming (not just a one-day spike) —
the cue to consider trimming into the historical fade.

| Analog | Plateau entry (GTA VI) | Half-confirm date |
|---|---|---|
| GTA V (120+, 3 td) | 2026-10-27 (Tue) | **2026-10-28 (Wed)** |
| RDR2 (120+, 3 td) | 2026-10-21 (Wed) | **2026-10-22 (Thu)** |
| RDR2 (118+, 8 td) | 2026-10-16 (Fri) | **2026-10-22 (Thu)** |

**2026-10-22** appears from both the RDR2 peak *and* the looser 118+ half-plateau
— the single most-supported "confirm-and-consider-trimming" date. GTA V's tighter
plateau pushes its confirm to **2026-10-28**.

## Bottom line / watch plan

- **Peak zone:** week of **Oct 22–29, 2026** (T-20 → T-15).
- **Confirmation:** a held ~120+-equivalent plateau gives a half-confirm around
  **Oct 22–28, 2026**; both analogs converge on **Oct 22** / **Oct 28**.
- This zone is an **exit-watch / trim window, not an add zone** — both games
  faded into launch. The release day itself was flat-to-down for both (GTA V
  −2.0%, RDR2 −0.5% on the T-1→T0 reaction), and the two split afterward —
  GTA V re-accelerated (+5.6% by +30 td) while RDR2 rolled over hard (−12.2% by
  +30 td). "Sell the news" is the base case; the pre-release plateau is the
  cleaner exit than the launch itself.
- Caveats: n=2; different market regimes (RDR2's post-launch fade overlapped the
  Q4-2018 selloff); GTA VI is only at T-78 now and hasn't entered the run-up ramp
  yet, so the "+23% by T-100+X" magnitude is the pattern to confirm, not a given.
