TTWO closed FY2026 (ended March 31, 2026) with GAAP net revenue of \$6,656.4M, up **+18.2%** Y/Y, and the growth is accelerating on a quarterly basis into the GTA VI launch: q2-2026 +31.1%, q3-2026 +24.9%. Net Bookings hit a record \$6.72B. Critically, FY2026 marked the **turn to positive free cash flow**: company FCF (OCF − PP&E) swung from −\$214.6M in FY2025 to **+\$461.5M** in FY2026, a +6.9% margin, as operating cash flow flipped from −\$45.2M to +\$624.3M. The first-half seasonality is real — q1-2026 FCF was −\$69.8M — but H2 (q3 +\$236M, q4 +\$199M) carried the year.

The catch is FCF *quality*. TTWO capitalizes ~\$689M/yr of software development that never touches the company-FCF line, and layers ~\$305M of SBC on top. On the strict ex-SBC/ex-all-capex basis, FCF is still deeply negative (**−\$532.7M**, −8.0% margin). So the headline "FCF-positive" story rests on treating game-development spend as capex rather than opex — defensible, but the conservative view says the business does not yet self-fund.

Valuation is full but not extreme for the setup. TTM **P/S 6.4x**, and the market is clearly pricing the GTA VI year: **forward P/S on FY guidance compresses to 5.3–5.4x** against the \$7.9–8.1B FY2027 revenue guide (+~20%). TTM **P/FCF is 92.7x** — optically expensive, and n/m on the strict basis. The guidance track record is excellent: TTWO beat and raised the FY2026 number every quarter (final actual +8.2% over the initial q1 guide), and each next-quarter guide came in ahead.

Balance sheet is healthy and improving: \$1.55B cash vs. \$2.52B debt (modest net debt), with debt down 31% Y/Y and management guiding to a **net cash position by end of FY2027**. The valuation is reasonable *if* GTA VI delivers the step-change; on trailing cash flow alone it is stretched.

## 3. News

The entire TTWO thesis now orbits one event: **Grand Theft Auto VI, confirmed for November 19, 2026** on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Rockstar opened **pre-orders on June 25, 2026** at a **premium \$79.99** price point, and shares rose ~3% on the pricing/date confirmation — investors read premium pricing as accretive to an already-record FY2027. Pre-order demand is tracking strong: **B. Riley (July 10)** flagged GTA VI as the best-selling item across monitored platforms since pre-orders opened, sustaining momentum into launch.

**Upcoming catalysts.** Fiscal **Q1 2027 earnings on August 7** (quarter ended June 30, 2026) is the next hard date, followed by **NBA 2K27 on September 4** and the GTA VI launch itself on November 19. Jefferies frames the August print as **likely in-line-to-soft** — Street bookings are modeled ~−4% Y/Y on **mobile weakness** (several Zynga titles maturing) — but expects little new GTA VI disclosure (no pre-order figures, no change to the \$8.0–8.2B FY2027 net-bookings guide). The near-term swing factor analysts keep citing is the **structure and timing of GTA Online**, which is both the biggest catalyst and the biggest risk.

**Sentiment / positioning.** Sell-side is overwhelmingly bullish: consensus **"Strong Buy," ~29 analysts, avg PT ~\$284** (range \$170–\$368) — roughly +23% above spot. July actions skew positive: **Wells Fargo raised to \$289 (Overweight)**, Jefferies reiterated Buy (Jul 14), and late-June saw BMO (\$285), BTIG initiating Buy (\$290), and Morgan Stanley lifting to \$265. ## Verdict

TTWO is a single-catalyst story: the November 19 launch of Grand Theft Auto VI at a premium \$79.99 sets up a record FY2027 (~20% net-bookings growth guided), FY2026 already turned the corner to positive company free cash flow (+\$461.5M), and the sell-side is near-unanimously bullish (Strong Buy, ~\$284 avg target). The offsets are a full trailing valuation (P/S 6.4x, P/FCF 92.7x, deeply negative FCF once capitalized game-dev and SBC are stripped out), a broken near-term price trend with overhead POC resistance around \$239, and softening mobile bookings against a weak industry backdrop (US game spending −21% Y/Y in June). The dominant risk is GTA VI itself — a title that has already slipped twice; a further delay or a weak GTA Online monetization structure would hit hardest, while the August 7 earnings print is expected to be a low-information, in-line-to-soft quarter. Net a modest BUY: the multi-year setup and analyst support outweigh a stretched trailing multiple and a soft tape, but position sizing should respect the elevated IV (±12.9%/mo) and binary launch risk. What would raise the rating: strong pre-order disclosure or a raised FY2027 outlook; what would lower it: any GTA VI date slippage or a bookings/guidance miss on August 7.
