TTWO's reported numbers are in the **trough before GTA VI**, and the table should be read that way. Q1-2027 revenue of \$1,533.9M grew only **+2.0% Y/Y**, a sharp deceleration from FY-2026's +18.2% (which was flattered by +31.1% and +24.9% quarters). This is the expected air pocket: the quarter ended June 30, capturing only five days of GTA VI pre-orders, and net bookings actually *fell* 3% to \$1.39B.

Cash flow is poor and structurally understated by the standard FCF line. TTM operating cash flow is \$500.2M and company FCF \$337.5M (+5.0% margin), but Q1-2027 alone burned **-\$193.8M of FCF** — normal seasonality for TTWO's Q1 (the year-ago quarter was -\$69.8M), though materially worse. The critical adjustment is **capitalized software of \$697.3M TTM**, which exceeds PP&E capex four-fold and is the real development spend for a game publisher. Including it plus \$350.6M of SBC, **strict ex-SBC FCF is -\$710.4M** (-10.6% margin). TTWO has not generated genuine free cash flow in years; FY-2025 strict FCF was -\$1,230.2M.

This makes the multiples nearly meaningless today. TTM P/S of **6.9x** is mid-range historically (4.7x to 7.5x over two years), but TTM P/FCF of **136.6x** and `n/m` strict simply reflect the pre-launch trough. The forward figures matter more: **FW P/S on FY guidance is 5.7–5.8x**, against FY-2027 net bookings guidance of **\$7.9–8.1B** — roughly +20% over FY-2026's \$6.66B.

Guidance is the live controversy. TTWO has beaten its next-quarter guide every quarter shown (+0.9% to +9.4%), yet **Q2 was guided to \$1,420–1,470M net bookings against ~\$1.79B consensus**, and FY-2027 reiterated at \$8.0–8.2B versus ~\$8.62B modelled. Management explicitly declined to raise on "unprecedented" pre-orders. Given the beat history, this reads as deliberate conservatism, but it is a real gap between guidance and the street.

Balance sheet: **\$1.36B cash against \$2.52B debt** (~\$1.2B net debt), improved from \$3.66B of debt a year ago. Share count is drifting up (175.2M → 187.0M, +6.7%).

Verdict: valuation is a pure function of a launch that hasn't happened; the trough numbers are ugly by design.

## 3. News

**Surface narrative.** Everything orbits **GTA VI on Nov 19, 2026**. Take-Two reported Q1-FY2027 on Aug 7 in an unprecedented slot — **Friday, 8am ET, before the open**, its first-ever pre-market report — which the community read as a signal in itself. The headline out of the call: pre-orders are "unprecedented and astonishing," but guidance was left unchanged.

**Depth pass — the release date and the guidance are hard; the pre-order euphoria is not.** The **Nov 19 date was formally reaffirmed** for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, as was an **Aug 27 Netflix "Extended Look"** premiere (YouTube six hours later). Those are committed, dated facts. The quarter itself is real and unspectacular: revenue \$1.53B (+2%), **net bookings -3% to \$1.39B**, a GAAP net loss of \$34.1M including a **\$43.4M impairment** for a cancelled third-party title, with recurrent consumer spending -1% at 84% of bookings. NBA 2K bookings +7% and GTA +3% were the disclosed drivers.

The pre-order commentary is where substance thins. Zelnick called the level "unprecedented," but **disclosed no unit or dollar figure**, and explicitly refused to raise guidance: *"we haven't sold one unit yet… you can cancel a pre-order."* That is management declining to convert enthusiasm into a number — which is honest, but it means "record pre-orders" is currently an uncorroborated management characterization. The nearest third-party data is **Newzoo's ~\$180M in digital pre-orders across the US and top-five European markets in the last week of June**, described as the strongest pre-order campaign ever recorded, projecting \$3.3–5.2B by launch week. That is a real estimate from an outside firm, but it is a model, not sales.

**The guidance gap is the concrete conflict.** Q2 net bookings guided to **\$1.62–1.67B against ~\$1.79B consensus**, and FY-2027 reiterated at **\$8.0–8.2B versus ~\$8.62B modelled**. Piper Sandler's framing — that TTWO habitually guides launch years conservatively — is supported by this repo's own data: the company beat its next-quarter guide every quarter for two years, by up to +9.4%. Analyst targets are wide (BofA \$368, Piper \$280, consensus ~\$295) and uniformly Buy-rated with no Holds, which is itself a crowded-sentiment warning.

**Price move vs. driver.** Shares sold off four consecutive sessions into the print, then fully recovered on the day (+13.00 from the open) — net flat. The market is not paying up in advance, and June already produced a textbook sell-the-news episode when pre-orders opened (-3% after +13% the prior week) on the \$79.99 price disappointing bulls hoping for \$90–100.

**Catalysts / risks.** Aug 27 Netflix look; NBA 2K27 on Sept 4; then the **Oct 22–29 pre-release plateau window** this repo's [note](../notes/pre_release_plateau.md) identifies as the historical exit zone; launch Nov 19. Risks, in order: **a delay** — GTA VI has slipped repeatedly and a fifth delay would be severe; digital-only, \$80 pricing and no physical disc; a launch-quality or server failure; and the fact that ~20% of FY bookings growth is loaded into a single title with no fallback.

**Rating:** +0.9 — BUY

## Verdict

TTWO is a single-catalyst position 103 days from the largest entertainment launch ever attempted, with the Nov 19 date reaffirmed and pre-orders management calls unprecedented — corroborated at least directionally by Newzoo's ~\$180M first-week estimate. The reported fundamentals are deliberately in a trough (revenue +2%, bookings -3%, strict ex-SBC FCF -\$710M once the \$697M of capitalized software is charged), so the 6.9x TTM sales multiple is doing no work; the 5.7x forward on \$7.9–8.1B FY guidance is the real number, and that guidance sits ~5% below consensus from a company that has beaten its own guide every quarter for two years. The dominant risk is a fifth delay, followed by launch-execution failure; a crowded all-Buy analyst book with no Holds is its own warning. The actionable structure is timing rather than thesis: this repo's plateau analog points to **Oct 22–29** as the historical peak-and-trim window, with both prior mega-launches fading into release day. Confirmation of an elevated plateau in that window would be the cue to trim; a delay announcement or a disappointing Aug 27 showing would break the setup outright.

**Rating:** +0.7 — BUY
