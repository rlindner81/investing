## Decision Notes

- **Thesis breaks if:** the FY NB guide is cut below \$8.0B at the low end with GTA VI 10 days out, which would imply weak pre-order conversion. Any change to the Nov 19 date is the extreme case.
- **Thesis confirms if:** FY \$8.0–8.2B and OCF over \$1.0B hold or rise, and management gives pre-order or sell-through color strong enough to start the FY2028 (\$8.51 EPS consensus) debate.
- **Valuation:** FW P/S on the FY guide is 5.0–5.1x, the cheapest in the two-year table, and TTM P/S is 6.0x. Q2 itself matters little; the market will trade the FY guide and launch commentary.
- **Drift:** after-close day-1 moves have held at +5 days in both directions (q2-2026: -8.1% → -5.9%; q3-2025: +14.0% → +14.8%), so don't count on a fade reversing the reaction bar.
- **Fiscal-Q2 history is split:** two up (+7.5%, +2.0%) and two down (-8.1%, -13.7%). Before Aug's +6.0%, TTWO had four straight negative day-1 reactions.
- **Levels:** support is the \$190 Oct 30 low. Overhead are the \$226–236 POC shelf and the \$232.47 pre-Q1 close. Short interest was 8.7M shares (4.6% of shares out) at Jun 30.
- **Next catalyst:** launch day (Nov 19) is T+7 trading days after the reaction bar. At the GTA V and RDR2 launches, the release bar was flat to down (-2.0% and -0.5%).
