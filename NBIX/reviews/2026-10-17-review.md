**Revenue is accelerating, partly by acquisition.** Q2-2026 revenue was \$959.0M, up 39.5% Y/Y, after +42.2% in Q1, against +21.4% for all of FY2025. TTM revenue is \$3.37B. Through Q1 this was organic: Ingrezza grew double digits and Crenessity ramped (\$184M in Q2, +20% Q/Q). Q2 adds roughly six weeks of Vykat XR following the Soleno acquisition closing on May 18, so from H2 the growth rate mixes organic and acquired revenue.

**FCF is solid but seasonal and lumpy.** TTM company FCF is \$870M (25.8% margin). Q1 and Q2 run thin (13–17%), and Q4 normally carries the year (FY2025 Q4 was \$391M of \$749M). Q2-2026 also absorbed Soleno transaction and integration costs. SBC is substantial at \$235M TTM, roughly 27% of FCF. That gap between company FCF (\$870M) and strict ex-SBC FCF (\$636M) matters, but it hasn't changed: share count has stayed around 99–102M for two years.

**The valuation looks undemanding.** At \$135.71 the market cap is \$13.8B: 4.1x TTM P/S, 15.8x P/FCF and 21.7x strict ex-SBC P/FCF. That is the lowest P/S and P/FCF in the whole display window, even though growth is the fastest it has been. The `FW P/S FY guidance` of 4.8–4.9x is an **Ingrezza-only** multiple and overstates the real forward multiple. Annualizing the H2 run-rate gives FY2026 total revenue of roughly \$3.6–3.8B, which puts forward P/S near \~3.7x.

**The guidance record is clean.** The Ingrezza guide was raised to \$2.825–2.875B from \$2.70–2.80B, and the FY2024 and FY2025 guides were met or beaten. Management doesn't guide total revenue or next-quarter revenue.

**The balance sheet is debt-free but thinner.** Debt is zero (the \$600M revolver draw was repaid inside Q2), but the \$2.9B Soleno deal used up a large part of the securities portfolio. A \$2.2B Vykat intangible now amortizes over 16 years, which will weigh on GAAP EPS.

**Verdict:** NBIX looks cheap for a debt-free, FCF-positive company growing \~40%. The market is clearly discounting Vykat safety risk and Ingrezza's IRA exposure later in the decade.

**Rating:** +1.2 — BUY

## 3. News

**Surface narrative.** One story dominates: **Vykat XR safety**. On August 12, STAT reported a physician alert citing 7 deaths and 100+ serious adverse events (mostly hospitalizations for edema, respiratory and cardiac problems) among Vykat-treated Prader-Willi patients, and the stock fell 5.8% that day. On October 13, a STAT+ follow-up built on FOIA'd FDA case reports quoted a former FDA medical officer calling for withdrawal. NBIX fell \~4.5% intraday the next session. Positive items got little attention: real-world evidence presentations on Vykat (Sep 30) and Crenessity/CAH (Oct 16), and the Q3 date announcement on Oct 12.

**Deeper read on Vykat: real data, unproven causality.** The adverse-event counts are genuine FAERS entries. FAERS is spontaneous reporting in a medically fragile population with high baseline mortality and polypharmacy, and it can't establish causation. The August experts explicitly did *not* call for withdrawal. The FDA has issued **no safety communication or label change** as of mid-October. The withdrawal call comes from one ex-regulator and is opinion. So the headline sits on real reports, but the price reaction assumes regulatory action that hasn't happened. On the substantiated side, Vykat sales were flat sequentially in Q2 (\~\$94M pro forma), and management guided Q3 flat as well because of discontinuations working through. Some of that is likely edema-related, and management declined to break it down.

**Core business: substantiated and strong.** The figures are in filings. Q2 revenue was \$959M (+39.5% Y/Y), H1 Ingrezza sales \$1.4B (+17%), Crenessity \$184M (+20% Q/Q, \~15% of diagnosed patients reached), and the Ingrezza guide was raised by \$100M at the midpoint. None of this is in dispute. The derating from \$166.80 to \$135.71 since the report has no fundamental negative behind it beyond the Vykat headlines.

**Structural overhang: real but distant.** Ingrezza loses its small-biotech IRA exemption and becomes negotiation-eligible later in the decade. About 60% of its volume is Medicare, and competitor Austedo's negotiated price, 38% below list from 2027, sets the precedent. The overhang is legitimate, but it's a known multiple compressor, not news. The DOJ civil investigative demand from August 2025 over Ingrezza marketing is still open.

**Catalysts.** The **Q3 report is Nov 2 after the close** (1 PM PT release, 4:30 PM ET call). Things to watch: the Vykat sales trajectory and discontinuation commentary, any label update, the Ingrezza guide, a first Crenessity guide, and whether any 2027 Ingrezza pricing color is given. A possible FDA safety action on Vykat is the binary risk.

**Sentiment.** Sell-side remains bullish: about 88% Buy, average target \~\$206. Short interest has climbed to 7.5% of float from 2.4% two years ago. JPMorgan has disclosed a 5.2% passive stake. Institutional money hasn't left, but shorts are leaning on the Vykat narrative.

**Key risks.** An FDA label change or REMS on Vykat would impair a \$2.9B acquisition. Other risks: Ingrezza IRA pricing, Austedo price competition, competition for Crenessity in CAH, and execution on Vykat integration.

**Rating:** -0.4 — HOLD

## Verdict

NBIX trades at its cheapest multiples in two years: 4.1x TTM sales, 15.8x FCF, and \~3.7x estimated FY2026 total revenue. Organic growth from Ingrezza and Crenessity is filed and raised. The stock has still fallen \~19% since the Q2 report, almost entirely on Vykat XR safety headlines that rest on real but non-causal FAERS data and haven't drawn any FDA action. Technically the trend is down, short interest is rising, and resistance sits overhead, so momentum and fundamentals point in opposite directions. The main risk is an FDA label or REMS action on Vykat. That would impair the \$2.9B Soleno deal and confirm the bear case on capital allocation. A clean Q3 on Nov 2 would move the rating toward BUY: steady Ingrezza, continued Crenessity growth, Vykat stabilizing and no regulatory escalation. A safety communication or an Ingrezza guide cut would move it toward SELL.

**Rating:** +0.0 — HOLD
