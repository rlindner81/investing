# Kinross Gold Corporation (NYSE: KGC) — Management Team

## Executive Leadership

---

### J. Paul Rollinson — Chief Executive Officer

**Background:**
- Chief Executive Officer of Kinross since **August 2012**
- Joined Kinross in 2012 as Executive Vice-President of Corporate Development shortly before being appointed CEO
- Prior to Kinross, spent much of his career in mining investment banking, including senior roles at Scotia Capital (Scotiabank), where he covered the global mining sector
- Background as a mining engineer, giving him both a technical and a capital-markets grounding

**Role at Kinross:**
- Has led the company through a decade-long portfolio transformation: divestment of higher-risk assets (Russia and Ghana exited in 2022), a sustained focus on operational reliability, cost discipline and free-cash-flow generation
- Under his tenure Kinross has strung together several years of meeting or beating production and cost guidance, and delivered record free cash flow in FY2025
- Primary voice on the strategic narrative and CEO commentary in each quarterly news release

## Corporate Governance Notes

- **Board Chair:** **Kelly J. Osborne**, a director since 2015, was approved as Chair of the Board effective upon his re-election at the **May 2025** annual meeting. Osborne brings deep mining-industry operating and senior-executive experience.
- **Chair transition:** Osborne succeeded **Catherine McLeod-Seltzer**, who served on the Board from 2005 and as Chair from 2019, and who retired at the May 2025 annual meeting after two decades of service.
- **Single-class shares:** Kinross has a conventional single class of common shares (one vote per share) — no dual-class or super-voting structure. Voting power tracks economic ownership, unlike founder-controlled technology issuers.
- **Ownership structure:** widely held; no controlling shareholder. The 30% non-controlling interest that appears in the financial statements relates to the **Manh Choh joint venture** (Kinross 70% / Contango–Peak Gold 30%), not to a share-class arrangement.
- **Filing status:** as a Canadian company under the U.S.–Canada Multijurisdictional Disclosure System (MJDS), Kinross files an annual **40-F** and furnishes interim results on **Form 6-K**, rather than filing 10-K/10-Q reports.
