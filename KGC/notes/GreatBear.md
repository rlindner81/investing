# Great Bear — Project Reference (KGC)

Kinross' flagship development project. High-grade gold at Red Lake, Ontario, Canada.
Acquired via the Great Bear Resources takeover (closed Feb 2022). Positioned as the
anchor of Kinross' grade-enhancement strategy for the 2030s (alongside Lobo-Marte).

**Status as of the July 2026 investor presentation** — see
`KGC-Investor-Presentation-July-2026.pdf` (slides 23–31). Nothing below is new vs. the
Q1 2026 earnings (reported 2026-04-29); the deck restates it.

## Current state

- **First production targeted late 2029**, subject to permitting.
- **Detailed engineering ~45% complete** (Q1/26). AEX (Advanced Exploration) decline
  construction to start summer 2026; all remaining AEX permits received.
- **Permitting is the gating item.** Federal final-phase Impact Statement submitted
  Q1/26. Kinross needs the final federal impact-assessment report plus certain
  provincial early-works/construction permits **by spring 2027** to use the 2027 summer
  construction season and hold the late-2029 first-production date. Provincial track runs
  under Ontario's "One Project, One Process." Indigenous benefits agreements (Lac Seul,
  Wabauskang First Nations) advancing on a signed confidential MOU.

## PEA economics — preliminary and STALE (use with heavy caveats)

Source: **Great Bear PEA published 2024-09-10.** A PEA (Preliminary Economic Assessment)
is the *lowest-confidence* study tier under NI 43-101 — it may include **inferred
resources** ("too geologically speculative to have economic considerations applied").
These are conceptual, **not bankable**, numbers.

All economics quoted **@ \$4,500/oz gold**, after-tax, 5% discount:

| Metric | PEA value |
|---|---|
| Avg annual production (first 8 yrs) | ~518 koz/yr (peak profile ~500–600 koz) |
| AISC | \$865/oz (LOM cash cost \$645/oz) |
| Initial capital | **\$1.4B** (incl. contingencies) |
| Initial mine life | 12 years |
| NPV | \$7,974M |
| IRR | 62.7% |
| Payback | <1 year |
| Mill | 10 ktpd conventional circuit, 95.7% recovery, >4 g/t mill grade |

## ⚠️ Catalyst / risk to watch — early 2027

On completion of **detailed engineering in early 2027**, Kinross will issue an
**updated initial-capital figure**. Management has explicitly warned it will come in
**higher** than the \$1.4B PEA estimate, driven by:

1. **Inflation** since the 2024 PEA (management flagged this on the Q1/26 call — "there
   will definitely be an inflation component"; applies to Lobo-Marte too).
2. **Scope enhancements** chosen during detailed engineering (e.g. beefed-up water
   management, "beyond standard practices").

**Do not treat the \$1.4B capex or the derived NPV/IRR/payback as current.** The early-2027
update is the next hard data point — a materially higher capex would compress returns
(though the \$4,500/oz price deck provides large cushion at current gold prices).

Great Bear is the **near-term driver** of Kinross' development capex ramp: AEX spend is
largely behind it (surface 93% complete, decline first-blasted 2026-07-27), and
main-project construction (~3-year build, 2027–2029) layers in ~\$550–670M/yr *starting*
2027 — the bulk after the next-4-quarter window. For the combined Great Bear + Lobo-Marte
spending trajectory (FY2026 ~\$1.5B guided → the FY2027–2028 inflection to \$2.5–3.0B+
peak) and what it does to FCF, see the dedicated [Capex Ramp](CapexRamp.md) note.

## Upside optionality

- Great Bear resource: **2.7 Moz M&I + 4.3 Moz Inferred** (Dec 31, 2025), up from the
  acquisition base — Inferred has grown every annual update (2,290 → 4,291 koz).
- Deep drilling below the PEA inventory continues to hit high grade (e.g. BR-770C3
  22.7m @ 6.51 g/t; BR-890A 1.6m @ 18.79 g/t), plus a new **Strider zone** 2.4 km on
  strike. AEX decline will open underground drilling access to extend the resource at
  depth. Management frames Great Bear as a potential **multi-decade** operation beyond
  the 12-year PEA base case.
