# Capex Ramp — Development Spending Outlook (KGC)

Kinross is entering a multi-year **development capex ramp** as its two flagship growth
projects — [Great Bear](GreatBear.md) (Ontario, first gold late 2029) and
[Lobo-Marte](LoboMarte.md) (Chile, first gold early 2030s) — move from study/permitting
into construction. This note frames the combined spending trajectory and the point at
which it stresses the self-funding model. It is the cross-project view; each project note
links here rather than duplicating the curve.

**Key caveat:** Kinross does **not** break capex out by project publicly — only a single
total guidance figure plus qualitative "which mines drove the increase" color. Everything
below is triangulated from the FY total guide, the disclosed build timelines, and the
run-rate. **Reassess each quarter** against actual capex and any re-cut figures.

## Anchors (as of Q2 2026, reported 2026-07-29)

- **FY2026 total capex guidance: \$1,500M ±5%** (attributable), reaffirmed at Q2.
- **H1-2026 actual: \$694.2M** (Q1 \$283.2M + Q2 \$411.0M) — ~46% of the full-year guide.
  → H2-2026 implied ≈ **\$806M**, i.e. **~\$400M/qtr**. The big sequential jump already
  happened (Q1→Q2 +45%, driven by Curlew, Round Mtn Phase X, Bald Mtn Redbird, Great Bear
  AEX, and Paracatu timing); H2 sustains that pace rather than re-accelerating.

## Next 4 quarters (Q3-2026 → Q2-2027) — the "ramp," manageable

| Quarter | Est. total capex | Driver |
|---|---|---|
| Q3-2026 | ~\$400M | Great Bear AEX + surface, Round Mtn Phase X, Paracatu |
| Q4-2026 | ~\$400–430M | same, year-end true-up |
| Q1-2027 | ~\$430–480M | Great Bear main-project engineering completes → early construction begins |
| Q2-2027 | ~\$480–550M | Great Bear construction ramps; Lobo-Marte early works may start if EIA progresses |
| **NTM total** | **~\$1.7–1.85B** | vs. ~\$1.4B trailing — a **\$300–450M step-up** |

At current realized gold (~\$4,483/oz Q2) this is comfortably self-funded: TTM OCF ~\$4.5B
absorbs the step-up, FCF stays solidly positive, net-cash position intact.

## The real inflection is FY2027–2028, NOT the next 4 quarters

- **FY2027 likely \$1.8–2.2B** — first full year with Great Bear *main* construction, plus
  rising Lobo-Marte engineering/procurement. The **early-2027 Great Bear capex re-cut**
  (management has pre-warned it lands above the \$1.4B PEA figure — see
  [Great Bear](GreatBear.md) § Catalyst) could push this materially higher.
- **FY2028–2029: peak, potentially \$2.5–3.0B+** if both projects overlap in heavy
  construction. This is the year that tests the self-funding model — stress case is peak
  capex *combined with* a gold pullback toward the \$3,500 deck.

## Per-project timing that shapes the curve

- **Great Bear** = the near-term driver. AEX spend largely behind (surface 93% complete,
  decline first-blasted 2026-07-27). Main-project construction is a ~3-year build
  (2027–2029, first gold late 2029) → ~\$550–670M/yr layered in *starting* 2027, mostly
  after the next-4-quarter window.
- **Lobo-Marte** = the later, larger one, but it barely touches the next 4 quarters. \$1.8B
  over 3 years, first gold **early 2030s**, gated on the **Chilean EIA (critical path)**.
  Next-4-quarter spend is pre-construction only (engineering, long-lead procurement, early
  works) — call it **\$50–150M**, not the heavy ~\$600M/yr build rate. Heavy Lobo-Marte
  spend is a **2028+ event**.

## FCF impact

The \$300–450M NTM step-up shaves ~\$0.25–0.30/share of FCF holding gold flat — immaterial
at current prices, meaningful only if gold falls while capex peaks. Kinross intends to
**self-fund both projects from operating cash flow** (Q2 2026: ~\$1.9B net cash, ~\$727M
attributable FCF in the quarter); the thesis holds while gold stays elevated and breaks
only in the FY2028 peak-capex + gold-pullback stress case.
