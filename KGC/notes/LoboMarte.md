# Lobo-Marte — Project Reference (KGC)

Kinross' second flagship development project, slated to follow Great Bear in the early
2030s. Long-life, low-cost heap-leach gold in Chile's Maricunga belt (Atacama region).
100%-owned since 2008; comprises the Lobo and Marte deposits. Positioned — alongside
[Great Bear](GreatBear.md) — as an anchor of Kinross' 2030s grade/production pipeline
and its runway in Chile into the 2040s.

**Status as of the 2026-07-29 project update** — furnished on Form 6-K the same evening
as Q2 2026 earnings (a *separate* news-release 6-K from the earnings release; EX-99.1 of
accession 0001171843-26-005024). This is a **high-level refresh of the 2021 feasibility
study (FS)**, updating economics for inflation and an enhanced execution strategy. It
delivers the "capex to be re-cut for inflation" event the [Great Bear](GreatBear.md) note
flagged as pending for Lobo-Marte.

## Project profile — long-life, low-cost heap leach

- **~15-year initial mine life**, ~4.6 Moz total production — based on the **2021 FS pit
  design, which only includes a portion of the total resource**.
- **~350 koz Au/yr** average at steady state.
- **AISC ~\$1,000/oz** — very low. Driven by a strong 1.3 g/t heap-leach grade, a low
  2.0:1 strip ratio, and a \$12.30/t processing cost. Copper recovered as a by-product
  (SART plant) nets against costs.
- Two sequential open pits (Lobo, then Marte), conventional truck-and-shovel, 50 Mtpa
  peak mining rate, \$3.25/t average mining cost, 69% average gold recovery. 35 ktpd
  three-stage crushing + ADR facility. 100% renewable grid power, electric shovels.

much lower gold-price environment (2021)** and do not capture the full mineralized system.

## Capital & funding

- **~\$1.8B initial capex** over three years — \$1,510M base + \$290M contingency
  (mining & equipment \$145M, site development/infrastructure \$410M, process facilities
  \$490M, heap leach \$70M, indirect/other \$395M).
- **Self-funded from operating cash flow** — consistent with Kinross' disciplined,
  debt-averse model (Q2 2026: ~\$1.9B net cash, ~\$727M attributable FCF in the quarter).
- ⚠️ This \$1.8B is the *high-level* refresh. Kinross will issue a **fulsome capital
  update once detailed engineering is substantially complete** — the next hard data point,
  and the figure to watch for further scope/inflation creep (same pattern as Great Bear).

**Capex timing — light near-term, heavy 2028+.** Lobo-Marte barely pressures the next 4
quarters: with first gold in the **early 2030s** and permitting (Chilean EIA) the critical
path, near-term spend is **pre-construction only** (engineering, long-lead procurement,
early works) — on the order of **\$50–150M** across the next four quarters, *not* the
heavy ~\$600M/yr build rate implied by \$1.8B over three years. The heavy Lobo-Marte draw
is a **2028+ event**, gated on the EIA. Great Bear is the nearer, bigger capex driver in
the meantime. For the combined project capex trajectory (FY2026 ~\$1.5B guided → the
FY2027–2028 inflection), see the dedicated [Capex Ramp](CapexRamp.md) note.

## Permitting & timeline — the critical path

- **EIA accepted for review by Chile's SEA (Environmental Assessment Service) in Q2 2026**;
  now advancing through the regulatory review process.
- Detailed engineering and procurement proceeding in parallel.
- **First gold production targeted early 2030s.**
- The **static 2021 FS mine plan is the base case for permitting** — deliberately held
  fixed because that's the plan advanced through baseline studies over prior years.

## Upside optionality

- Reserves/resources (Dec 31, 2025): **6.7 Moz P&P reserves** (160.7 Mt @ 1.30 g/t) plus
  **2.75 Moz Indicated** (120.8 Mt @ 0.71 g/t) and **0.67 Moz Inferred** (32.9 Mt @ 0.63
  g/t), resources exclusive of reserves.
- The current pit shells leave a portion of the resource outside, and Lobo-Marte has the
  character of a **large gold-copper porphyry system**, open at depth. Management flags
  reserve conversion, pit expansion and regional exploration upside as gold prices,
  technical work and future drilling get incorporated — potential to extend well beyond
  the 15-year base case.
