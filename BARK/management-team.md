# BARK, Inc. (NYSE: BARK) — Management Team

## Executive Leadership

---

### Matt Meeker — Co-Founder, CEO & Executive Chairman

**Background:**
- Prior to BARK, co-founded **Meetup.com**, the social networking platform for in-person group gatherings
- Served as Entrepreneur-in-Residence at DogPatch Labs (startup incubator run by Polaris Ventures)
- Active in the New York startup ecosystem as board member and advisor to multiple early-stage companies
- Serves as Venture Partner at Resolute VC

**Building BARK:**
- Co-founded BARK in October 2011, launching BarkBox as the initial product — a monthly themed subscription box for dogs
- Led the company through the June 2021 NYSE listing via SPAC merger with Northern Star Acquisition Corp. at ~$1.6B enterprise value
- Has steered the company through a strategic pivot away from pure subscription toward a broader omnichannel dog-lifestyle brand

**Role:**
- CEO and Executive Chairman — primary spokesperson on earnings calls and public-facing strategic communications
- Has maintained operational involvement since founding; represents continuity of the original brand vision

## Corporate Governance Notes

- **Founding structure:** BARK was co-founded by Matt Meeker, Henrik Werdelin (founder of Prehype, a venture studio), and Carly Strife (early Uber employee who launched Uber's New York operations). Werdelin and Strife are not active in day-to-day operations.
- **SPAC history:** The 2021 NYSE listing via SPAC means BARK lacks the IPO bookrunner credibility that a traditional institutional roadshow provides; the SPAC vehicle also contributed to an initially diluted shareholder base and the convertible debt structure that was subsequently retired.
- **Headquarters:** New York City, with operations distributed across the U.S.
- **Share repurchase:** Board authorized a share repurchase program; the company repurchased 6M shares for $10.5M in Q4 FY2025.
