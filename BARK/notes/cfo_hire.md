# BARK — the Anya Hamill CFO hire

**Anya Hamill starts 2026-09-08**, reporting to Meeker, succeeding interim CFO
Brian Dostie (a year in the seat, now back to VP Accounting & Controller). Comp:
\$450K base, 75% target bonus, paid half cash / half **immediately vested,
unrestricted shares**.

**Read the background against the strategy, not the org chart.** WhiteWave
2003–2017 (Silk, So Delicious), Danone premium yogurt, CFO of Little Secrets,
then CFO of Laird Superfood 2022–2026 — where the company went from roughly
−\$24M on \$35M of sales to positive cash flow on \$43M. That is food CPG top to
bottom, and it looks like a mismatch only if you think BARK is a subscription-box
company. It isn't: **consumables are ~⅓ of revenue and the stated primary growth
priority**, and the toys/accessories line is the one being de-emphasized because
it is China-sourced and tariff-exposed (~\$4M H1-FY2026 hit) while consumables
are domestic. The hire is aimed squarely at the segment BARK is betting on.

**Don't confuse consumables with BARK in the Belly.** That sub-brand donates 100%
of food profits and is explicitly not a margin contributor. The consumables
*line* is a different thing entirely.

**The opportunity is the refill annuity.** Licksters is a razor/razor-blade
design — durable refillable toy, recurring treat refills — seeded through
subscriber boxes first, then Target/PetSmart/Walmart/Amazon/Chewy. The
subscription base functions as the discovery and seeding layer for consumables
that monetize on repeat. That works *because* the cohort is stable: retention
+170bps Y/Y, AOV +\$0.45, LTV near a public-company high. Shrinking D2C revenue
is a lower entry point, not decay.

**The constraint:** subs can't shrink forever without eroding the launchpad every
consumables product depends on. Managed decline is not a stable end state.

**The risk is her equity.** Immediately vested shares are cash-like — weaker
alignment than multi-year vesting, though BARK had little negotiating leverage
and less cash to spend. Section 16 makes sales public on **Form 4 within two
business days**, which is soft pressure to hold. Watch for a 10b5-1 adoption or
early sales; holding through the [Q3-FY2027 test](fiscal_q3_inflection.md) is the
quiet confirmation.
