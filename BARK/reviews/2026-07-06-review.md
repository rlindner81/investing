BARK's revenue trajectory is deeply concerning: TTM revenue of \$394.8M represents an -18.5% Y/Y decline for FY-2026, with the rate of decline accelerating each quarter (Q1: -11.5%, Q2: -15.2%, Q3: -22.1%, Q4: -25.0%). This follows an already weak FY-2025, where revenue fell -1.2% to \$484.2M from the prior year. The business is shrinking rapidly with no visible bottom in sight.

FCF is solidly negative. Company FCF for TTM is -\$26.6M, worse than FY-2025's -\$13.2M, suggesting the cash burn is accelerating alongside revenue decline. Strict ex-SBC FCF is even worse at -\$40.9M TTM, with SBC running \$14.4M annually against a market cap of only \$92M — a meaningful dilution burden relative to size. The only FCF-positive quarters in the history shown were isolated (q2/q3-2025), and recent results have been uniformly negative.

At a TTM P/S of 0.2x and enterprise value of roughly \$73M (after \$19M net cash), valuation looks statistically cheap. But this is a value trap scenario: a 0.2x multiple on a business burning \$26M/year in FCF with accelerating revenue declines implies a shrinking denominator, not a cheap one. P/FCF is n/m throughout, as FCF has been negative for six consecutive quarters.

Guidance transparency has collapsed: FY revenue guidance has been withheld for multiple quarters, and the company only occasionally provides next-quarter ranges. The one quarter they provided NQ guidance with a comparison showed a -8.5% miss (Q4-2025 vs. Q3-2025 guidance of \$121–131M, actual \$115.4M). The absence of FY guidance is itself a red flag about management conviction.

Net cash of \$19M is thin against a \$26.6M annual FCF burn — at current pace, the company burns through its cash cushion within roughly nine months.

**Verdict:** BARK is a cash-burning, revenue-declining subscription pet business trading at a distressed multiple with an eroding balance sheet and no visible guidance anchor.
