Revenue is in an accelerating structural decline. Year-over-year growth turned negative in q1-2026 at -11.5% and worsened each quarter through q4-2026 at -25.0%, bringing FY-2026 to \$394.8M versus \$484.2M in FY-2025 (-18.5%). The bleeding continues into FY-2027: management guided \$325–340M (another ~15–17% Y/Y decline), making this three consecutive years of contraction with no inflection visible.

FCF is negative across every reported period. TTM company FCF is -\$26.6M and strict ex-SBC FCF is -\$40.9M, meaning SBC (\$14.4M TTM) absorbs more than half of the already negative company FCF. There are no quarters in the dataset where the business was sustainably FCF-positive at the company level; the two isolated positive quarters (q4-2026 \$1.6M, q3-2025 \$1.0M) were single-quarter anomalies. P/FCF is not meaningful anywhere in the table.

At 0.2x P/S on TTM revenue, the stock trades at a distressed multiple that reflects exactly this profile. Forward P/S at \$325–340M guidance is ~0.27x — still superficially cheap, but cheap relative to what? A business burning \$26M/year in cash with only \$19M on the balance sheet has less than one year of runway at the current rate. The \$40M share repurchase authorization is arithmetically incoherent against \$19M of net cash — likely a capital-allocation signal that relies on either future EBITDA improvement or unannounced financing.

Guidance trajectory is poor. FY full-year guidance was withheld in every quarter of FY-2026 after being given at q4-2025 (\$490–500M, subsequently missed by \$95–100M). The one piece of positive execution: management cut \$55M in costs and delivered \$0.2M in adjusted EBITDA for FY-2026, targeting \$7–10M for FY-2027. That is a real operational achievement but it does not arrest the revenue decay.

Net cash has fallen from \$78M (q2-2025) to \$51M (q4-2025) to \$19M (q4-2026), a \$59M drawdown in twelve months.

**Verdict:** Revenue in confirmed structural decline, FCF deeply negative, cash runway under one year — the balance sheet does not support the buyback authorization, and every guidance revision has been a cut.

## 3. News & Catalysts

### Q4 FY2026 Earnings — Revenue Miss, Weak FY2027 Guidance (June 9, 2026)

BARK reported Q4 FY2026 revenue of \$86.6M against a consensus estimate of roughly \$98M — an 11.6% miss — and full-year FY2026 revenue of \$394.8M (-18.5% Y/Y). The stock fell sharply after the report; from the report-date reference price of \$9.87 the stock briefly rallied toward \$16 in mid-June before retreating to \$10.40 today, a net loss of ~-4% from report date but with severe intraday swings. The Q4 miss was the headline negative: management acknowledged subscription attrition and price-sensitive customer churn as the primary drivers.

### FY2027 Guidance — Another Leg Down in Revenue

Management guided FY2027 revenue of \$325–340M, implying a further ~15–17% decline from FY2026's \$394.8M. On the EBITDA front, the company expects \$7–10M in adjusted EBITDA for FY2027, up from \$0.2M in FY2026 — the first year with a meaningful EBITDA target. The revenue compression paired with EBITDA improvement reflects an ongoing shift from growth to profitability via aggressive cost reduction (\$55M removed from the cost base in FY2026 across G&A, shipping, fulfillment, and marketing).

### \$40M Share Repurchase Authorized

BARK authorized a \$40M share repurchase program — notable given the current market cap of only ~\$90M and net cash of just \$19M. The buyback is likely funded by expected FY2027 operating improvement and/or the company's post-debt-payoff cash generation potential, but at current FCF burn rates it cannot be fully executed from existing cash.

### Reverse Stock Split — NYSE Compliance (April 1, 2026)

BARK executed a 1-for-20 reverse stock split effective April 1, 2026, to regain compliance with NYSE's minimum \$1 bid price requirement. This is a distress-flag event: the pre-split price had fallen to approximately \$0.50. While the mechanics are neutral, it signals the severity of the prior share price decline and may suppress institutional demand.

### BARK Air — Bright Spot

BARK Air revenue grew 138% Y/Y in Q2 FY2026, and commerce and air now represent 21% of total revenue (up from 15% in FY2025). This diversification is a genuine positive narrative, though the absolute dollar contribution remains small relative to the subscription drag.

### Debt-Free Balance Sheet

BARK cleared its \$45M convertible note during FY2026, ending the year with a clean balance sheet (\$19M cash, zero debt). This removes a key refinancing risk but the net cash position is thin relative to the FCF burn rate.

### Short Interest & Analyst Posture

Short interest was reported near 30% as of late 2025 — consistent with the extreme IV readings and large short-squeeze spikes visible in the price history. Analyst price targets appear varied and partially distorted by the reverse split, but consensus has moved sharply lower since FY2026 results.

### Upcoming Catalysts

- **Q1 FY2027 earnings**: Likely August 2026 — will confirm whether the revenue decline is decelerating toward FY2027 guidance or continuing to miss.
- **Buyback execution**: Any disclosure of open-market repurchases will be watched closely given the buyback-vs-cash mismatch.
- **BARK Air updates**: Continued hyper-growth in the air segment could reframe the narrative from subscription-melting-ice-cube to diversified pet-services platform.
- **Cash position**: If FCF does not improve materially in Q1 FY2027, the balance sheet becomes critical within 2–3 quarters.

## Rating

**SELL**

Revenue is in an accelerating multi-year decline with FY2027 guidance pointing to yet another ~15–17% drop, FCF is deeply negative at -\$26.6M TTM, and net cash of \$19M covers less than one year of burn at the current run rate — the \$40M buyback authorization is aspirational, not executable from existing liquidity. The 1-for-20 reverse split in April signals the degree of shareholder value destruction already realized, and the 200-day SMA (~\$13.30) and 2-year volume POC (~\$15.7) both represent significant overhead resistance. The adjusted EBITDA target of \$7–10M for FY2027 is a positive operational signal, as is BARK Air's 138% Y/Y growth, but neither meaningfully offsets the risk that cash runs out before the business reaches self-funding FCF; any equity raise would be highly dilutive from here. A rating change to Neutral or Buy would require Q1 FY2027 revenue materially above guidance, a quarter of positive company FCF, and evidence the buyback is being executed.
