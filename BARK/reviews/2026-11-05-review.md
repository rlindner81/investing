**Revenue: still shrinking, but the decline is easing.** Q2-FY2027 revenue was \$87.6M, down 18.1% Y/Y. That's the second straight quarter where the decline rate improved (−25.0% → −23.4% → −18.1%), and the quarter beat the top of the \$83–85M guide by 3%. The mix matters more than the headline. **Commerce grew 3.6%** to \$25.7M, its first growth in a while, and is now 29% of sales. DTC fell 25% to \$61.8M, which is the planned marketing pullback, while retention rose to 94% and AOV ticked up. The Q3 guide of \$88–92M implies −6.5% to −10.6% Y/Y against \$98.4M, so the decline keeps narrowing into the holiday quarter. The reaffirmed FY \$325–340M implies a Q4 of only \~\$69–84M. That looks conservative, though part of the gap is BARK Air (\~\$3.5M/qtr) leaving the P&L on Jan 1 if that sale closes.

**FCF: breakeven on the company definition, negative once SBC is counted.** Q2 company FCF was +\$4.6M, which pulls TTM to +\$0.5M from −\$24M a quarter ago. Much of that swing comes from lapping last year's heavy working-capital drain, a \$13.8M H1-FY2026 inventory build, rather than from operations alone. Inventory was flat sequentially this quarter, and management says it will release inventory in H2. Strict FCF ex-SBC is still −\$11.5M TTM. **SBC of \$11.9M TTM equals \~16% of the \$75M market cap**, so dilution is the real cost of keeping this company running.

**Valuation: priced like it's being liquidated.** It trades at 0.2x TTM sales and 0.2x the FY guide. \$19.0M of cash and no debt cover \~25% of the cap, so EV is \~\$56M, or \~0.16x sales. P/FCF of 160x is meaningless at breakeven.

**Guidance track record is clean since the reset.** The company beat its last three next-quarter guides (+1.0%, +4.2%, now +3%–4% above the high end), after the −8.5% and −4.0% misses that led to FY guidance being withheld through FY2026. FY2027 guidance has been held at \$325–340M for three straight reports.

**Verdict:** a deep-value, shrinking-but-stabilizing business where the market pays almost nothing for a now-cash-neutral operation. The bull case requires the Q3 decline to land in the single digits, and SBC dilution keeps this from being an outright bargain.

**Rating:** 0.6 — BUY

## 3. News

**Surface narrative.** Three things moved the story over the past six weeks: (1) **GNK Holdings' activism**, an open letter on Sep 15 and on Sep 25 a call for non-binding interest in a potential **\$11.00/share cash tender**, which sent shares up >20% premarket before the gain faded; (2) tonight's **Q2-FY2027 beat**; (3) a **preliminary deal to sell BARK Air's assets** and license the brand from Jan 1, 2027.

**Depth pass.**

- **Q2 beat: substantiated.** It's in the 10-Q and the 8-K: revenue \$87.6M vs a \$83–85M guide, adjusted EBITDA \$2.0M (+\$3.4M Y/Y), Commerce +3.6%, retention 94% vs 92%, cash +\$2.9M to \$19.0M, no debt, \$2.0M of buybacks. The Q3 guide of \$88–92M and the reaffirmed FY are committed numbers. One caveat: H1 DTC gross margin includes a **\$5.2M IEEPA tariff refund** tied to FY2026, so part of the margin story is one-off.
- **BARK Air exit: real direction, no committed terms.** It's disclosed in the release but described as a "preliminary understanding," with no buyer, price, or definitive agreement. It contributed \$3.5M of Q2 revenue. Dropping a capital-hungry novelty to fund Commerce and Licksters makes strategic sense, but treat it as intent until there's an 8-K.
- **GNK \$11 tender: mostly attention so far.** GNK solicited only non-binding indications by Oct 9 and said it had not decided to launch. **No Schedule TO appears in BARK's EDGAR feed through today**, almost four weeks past that deadline. The \$22 (split-adjusted) January bid was rejected by the Special Committee in March. Until a tender is filed, \$11 is a sentiment anchor about 32% above the price, not a floor, and the stock's slide to \$7.40 after the announcement shows the market isn't counting on it either.
- **Management turnover: substantiated, low signal.** CFO Anya Hamill started Sep 8. Former interim CFO Brian Dostie resigned as principal accounting officer on Oct 9 (8-K, no disagreements), and Hamill now holds both roles, which thins the finance bench in the run-up to the holiday quarter. Insider filings since August are tax-withholding Form 4s and small Form 144 sales by departed director McGinty, so there's no informative buying or selling.

**Price moves since Q1.** August +8.1% on fading volume, September −21.7% even with the tender headline (the GNK pop faded completely), October −6.1% to an all-time low. None of these moves came with new financial data, so the selloff looks like exhaustion and abandonment rather than a reaction to news.

**Catalysts.** The post-earnings reaction on Nov 6. The transcript is not out yet, so call color on the Q3 retail orders and BARK Air terms is still pending. A definitive BARK Air agreement before Jan 1. Any GNK Schedule TO filing. **Q3-FY2027 in \~early Feb 2027** is the decisive holiday-quarter test (see [fiscal_q3_inflection](../notes/fiscal_q3_inflection.md)).

**Sentiment.** Short interest is 10.6% of float with **12.6 days to cover**, a heavy load for this float that could produce an outsized move on a beat in either direction. Institutional coverage is thin, and the "Strong Buy / \$15.50 target" consensus is stale and detached from the price.

**Risks.** DTC still −25% Y/Y. SBC of \~16% of market cap a year. The H2 implied by the FY guide has a soft low end. Tariff exposure on China-sourced toys. The Licksters retail sell-through is unproven.

**Rating:** 0.3 — HOLD

## Verdict

BARK's Q2 is the first print where the numbers support the turnaround thesis. The revenue decline eased to −18% from −23%, revenue beat the guide's top end, Commerce grew again, company FCF swung to +\$4.6M, and cash rose with no debt. All of that sits on a \~\$56M enterprise value, or about 0.16x sales. The Q3 guide of −6.5% to −10.6% Y/Y keeps the decline narrowing into the holiday quarter. The BARK Air exit and GNK's \$11 tender idea give optional upside, but neither is committed: there's no definitive agreement and no Schedule TO. The main risk is that the stabilization relies on lapping last year's inventory build and one-off tariff refunds while DTC keeps shrinking \~25% a year, and SBC of \~16% of market cap annually dilutes holders even at breakeven FCF. The rating moves to BUY if Q3 lands at or above the \$90M midpoint with positive company FCF, or if GNK files a tender. It drops back to SELL if Q3 misses, or if the price fails at the \$8.64–8.95 resistance and returns to the \$7.40 low.

**Rating:** 0.2 — HOLD
