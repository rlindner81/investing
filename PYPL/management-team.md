# PayPal Holdings, Inc. (NASDAQ: PYPL) — Management Team

*Leadership as of mid-2026, following the February 2026 CEO transition.*

## Executive Leadership

---

### Enrique Lores — President & Chief Executive Officer

**Appointment:**
- Appointed President and CEO effective **March 1, 2026**, succeeding Alex Chriss.
  The Board announced the change on February 3, 2026 alongside Q4 2025 results.
- Had served on PayPal's Board for nearly five years and as **Board Chair since July
  2024**, so he stepped up from the boardroom to the operating role.
- Jamie Miller (CFO/COO) served as interim CEO during the brief transition before
  Lores assumed the role.

**Background:**
- More than 30 years in the technology industry, almost entirely at **HP / HP Inc.**,
  where he served as **President and CEO from November 2019** until moving to PayPal.
- At HP he led the company through a strategic transition, expanding beyond its
  traditional PC and printing roots into services, subscriptions, and AI-enabled
  "future of work" offerings — a track record of disciplined execution and complex
  transformation the PayPal Board explicitly cited.
- Reputation as a customer-centric, operationally disciplined leader.

**Mandate at PayPal:**
- The Board changed CEOs because "the pace of change and execution was not in line
  with the Board's expectations," after Q4 2025 revenue missed and FY2026 EPS guidance
  implied a decline. Lores's charge is sharper execution — especially reaccelerating
  **branded checkout** — while navigating an industry being reshaped by AI, changing
  regulation, and intense competition. On arrival he acknowledged: "We have significant
  strengths, but we have not executed to our full potential."

---

### Jamie Miller — Executive Vice President, Chief Financial & Operating Officer

**Role and tenure:**
- Joined PayPal as **CFO in November 2023**, one of the first senior hires by then-CEO
  Alex Chriss.
- Elevated to the additional role of **Chief Operating Officer in February 2025**,
  taking on broad operational responsibility alongside finance.
- Served as **interim CEO** during the Feb–Mar 2026 transition, then resumed her
  CFO/COO role under Lores.
- Primary financial spokesperson on earnings calls; presents results and the EPS /
  transaction-margin-dollar guidance framework.

**Background:**
- Deep large-cap finance and operations pedigree:
  - **Global CFO of EY (Ernst & Young)** — joined to lead the planned separation and
    IPO of EY's strategy/tax/consulting business.
  - **CFO of Cargill** (June 2021–January 2023), also owning corporate strategy and
    business development.
  - **12 years at General Electric**, most recently **SVP & CFO of GE**, where she led
    stabilization and deleveraging during GE's turnaround; earlier **CEO of GE
    Transportation** and GE's CIO.
  - Earlier: **Partner at PricewaterhouseCoopers**; senior roles at **Anthem, Inc.**
    (Controller, Investor Relations).
- **Education:** B.S. in Accounting, Miami University (1990).

### Alex Chriss — Former President & CEO (September 2023 – February 2026)

**Context (relevant to the covered quarters):**
- Led PayPal for the bulk of the period documented in this repo (Q4 2023 through
  Q4 2025 reports). Departed as part of the February 2026 leadership change; his
  separation costs (severance + accelerated RSUs) appear as a one-time item in the
  Q1 2026 results.

**Background:**
- Joined PayPal from **Intuit**, where he ran the **Small Business & Self-Employed
  Group** (including QuickBooks and the Mailchimp acquisition/integration).
- As PayPal CEO he: reframed investor communication around **transaction margin
  dollars** rather than headline revenue; **stopped issuing revenue-dollar guidance**
  (shifting to EPS and transaction-margin-dollar growth outlooks — a key convention
  reflected in this repo's FINANCIALS.yml guidance hints); pushed branded-checkout
  modernization (Fastlane), Venmo monetization, and the "agentic commerce" strategy;
  and initiated PayPal's **first quarterly dividend (Q3 2025)** alongside continued
  large buybacks.
- His tenure improved profitability and per-share metrics but did not reaccelerate
  branded-checkout growth to the Board's satisfaction, leading to the CEO change.

### Dan Schulman — Former President & CEO (2014 – September 2023)

- Led PayPal through the **2015 eBay spin-off** and the subsequent growth era,
  including the pandemic-driven surge in digital payments and major acquisitions
  (Honey, Hyperwallet, iZettle, Paidy). Retired in September 2023, handing off to
  Alex Chriss. Included here for historical continuity of the covered financials.

## Board & Governance Notes

- **Independent Board Chair:** **David W. Dorman** (former AT&T Chairman & CEO) was
  appointed Independent Board Chair effective February 3, 2026, separating the chair
  role from the CEO after Enrique Lores moved from Chair to CEO.
- **Single class of common stock:** Unlike founder-controlled tech peers (e.g., Snap's
  dual-class structure), PayPal has a single class of common stock — one share, one
  vote — with no controlling shareholder. Governance is board-driven, which is why an
  underperforming CEO can be (and was) replaced.
- **Capital return discipline:** PayPal returns substantial capital via buybacks
  (~\$6B/yr, steadily shrinking the share count from ~1.15B in 2022 to under 900M by
  Q1 2026) and, since Q3 2025, a quarterly dividend.
- **Leadership turnover:** Two CEO transitions in roughly 2.5 years (Schulman →
  Chriss → Lores) is the central governance/execution theme — the Board has shown it
  will act on execution shortfalls, but frequent turnover is itself an investor risk.
- **Headquarters:** San Jose, California.
