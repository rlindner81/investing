# T-Mobile US, Inc. (NASDAQ: TMUS) — Management Team

T-Mobile changed CEO on **November 1, 2025**: Mike Sievert handed over to Srini Gopalan, a Deutsche Telekom veteran who had joined T-Mobile as COO earlier in 2025. Sievert stayed on as Vice Chairman. Several senior roles were reshuffled around the handover, and Gopalan brought in former Deutsche Telekom colleagues. The team below reflects the Q2-2026 earnings call (July 23, 2026). Bios come from company releases and the earnings call transcripts under `quarters/`; details marked "reported" have not been checked against a proxy statement.

---

## Srini Gopalan — President and Chief Executive Officer

**Role:** CEO since November 1, 2025; member of the Board. COO of T-Mobile from early 2025 until the handover.

**Background:**
- Long Deutsche Telekom career. Most recently a Member of the DT Board of Management responsible for **Telekom Deutschland** (the German home market), and before that for DT's European segment.
- Earlier career (reported) at Vodafone, Bharti Airtel, Capital One and Hindustan Unilever, giving him consumer marketing and telecom operating experience in India, the UK and Europe.

**Notable:**
- As the first DT insider to run TMUS since the Sprint merger, he ties the subsidiary more closely to the controlling shareholder
- Led the February 2026 Capital Markets Day update, which moved headline KPIs from phone net adds to **postpaid accounts / ARPA**
- Hosts the earnings Q&A himself (submissions via X to @SriniGopalan)

## Peter Osvaldik — Executive Vice President and Chief Financial Officer

**Role:** CFO since 2021; runs guidance, capital allocation and the shareholder-return program.

**Background:**
- Has worked at T-Mobile for over a decade, in senior finance roles (SVP Finance, Chief Accounting Officer) before becoming CFO
- Earlier career (reported) in public accounting

**Notable:**
- Architect of the 2022–2026 shareholder-return framework (\$54.6B returned through June 2026) and the introduction of the dividend in Q3/Q4-2023
- Stays on as CFO across the CEO transition, which provides continuity in guidance methodology (Core Adjusted EBITDA and Adjusted FCF, no revenue guide)

## Mike Sievert — Vice Chairman (former President and CEO)

**Role:** CEO April 2020 – October 31, 2025; now Vice Chairman of the Board.

**Background:**
- Joined T-Mobile in 2012 as Chief Marketing Officer and was a key architect of the "Un-carrier" repositioning under John Legere; later President and COO
- Prior roles (reported) in consumer tech and telecom marketing, including Clearwire and Microsoft

**Notable:**
- Led the Sprint integration and the 5G network lead that drove 2020–2025 share gains
- Still appears on calls in a strategic role (he presented at the February 2026 Capital Markets Day update)

## Corporate Governance Notes

- **Controlled company:** Deutsche Telekom AG holds a majority of the voting power and has a stockholder agreement that gives it board nomination rights. **Timotheus Höttges** (DT CEO) chairs the board. T-Mobile uses the NASDAQ controlled-company exemptions.
- **SoftBank:** received \~48.8M contingent consideration shares in December 2023 (Sprint merger earn-out) and sold most of its remaining stake in 2025, removing a big overhang
- **Single share class** (no dual-class structure), but DT's control limits minority influence over strategy and capital allocation
- **Capital returns:** \$18.2B stockholder-return authorization through December 31, 2026 (buybacks plus dividends); quarterly dividend since December 2023
- **CEO succession** was planned and executed internally from the DT bench, which points to continuity of the network-plus-value strategy rather than a reset
